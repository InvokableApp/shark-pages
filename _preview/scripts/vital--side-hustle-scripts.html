How to follow up with your Side Hustle Quiz leads

Use these scripts immediately after receiving a new side hustle quiz lead.

Side Hustle Quiz Leads
Watch first 1

Watch this before you start calling.

Do this, in order 3
  1. 1

    Call them

    They probably won't pick up, so leave this voicemail:

    Hey [Name], it's [Your Name]!

    I saw you took our "What Side Hustle Is Right For You?" quiz, and Vital Health came up as your result.

    What I think is really interesting is how simple the sharing side can be. You have wellness products to share, your own referral links, and a compensation plan with multiple ways to earn based on product sales and building a customer or affiliate network. If you're open to it, I can text you a quick overview.

    If you're curious, just reply back to my text and I'll send you the details. No pressure at all!

    If you don't see my text, check your Unknown Senders or Spam folder. On an iPhone, look for the filter or menu icon in the top right corner of your Messages app. On an Android, look for the three-dot menu. You should be able to find any filtered messages there, which is where my text might be.

    Okay, that's all for now! Let me know if you got the text. Talk soon!

  2. 2

    Text them

    As soon as you hang up, send this:

    Hey [Name], it's [Your Name]! 👋 Just left you a quick voicemail. I saw you took the "What Side Hustle Is Right For You?" quiz, and Vital Health came up as your result.

    Quick question - would you be open to seeing how the compensation plan works? What caught my attention is that you can simply share wellness products through your own referral links, while the plan includes multiple ways to earn from product sales and building a customer or affiliate network. I can send you a quick overview if you're curious.

    If you're curious, just reply INFO and I'll send you the quick overview!

  3. 3

    Follow up in 24 hrs

    If they don't respond within 24 hours, text again:

    [Name]! Just following up on my text above! 👆 You probably haven't had a chance to see the Vital Health compensation plan yet, but it's actually one of the things that makes this opportunity pretty interesting. There are multiple ways to earn by sharing the products and growing a customer or affiliate network.

    If you want, I can send you a quick look at how it works. Just text me back INFO and I'll send it over!

Tip: Personalize [Name] and [Your Name] every time. Keep it conversational and focus on the simple sharing model and compensation plan rather than trying to explain everything at once.
