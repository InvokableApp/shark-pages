The Pre-Workout Bar

12 caffeine-free pre-workout drinks you make from real food

12Drinks

Why no caffeine

Most pre-workouts lean on caffeine. That's fine until you train after work and lie awake at midnight, or you're already three coffees in by the time you get to the gym.

These are 12 drinks you make from fruit, vegetables and a few things from the pantry, with no caffeine in any of them. Most take 5 minutes and a blender.

When to drink them

20 to 30 minutes beforeThat's the juices, spritzers and coolers. They're light, so they won't sit heavy once you start moving.
45 to 60 minutes beforeThe three smoothies. They're closer to a small meal, so give them a little longer.
Training first thingGo with a juice or a shot. A smoothie on the way out the door is a lot to move with.

Beet Berry Shot

A small, thick shot of beet and berries. It's earthy, sweet and gone in two sips.

5 minTo make
20 to 30 minBefore you train
2 shotsMakes

What goes in

  • ½ cup cooked beet, chopped
  • ½ cup frozen mixed berries
  • ½ cup cold water
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice (half a lemon)

Make it

01Blend everything until it's completely smooth, about 45 seconds.
02Pour a shot glass and drink it. Keep the second in the fridge for tomorrow, or strain it if you'd like it thinner.

In the glassThe berries take the edge off the beet's earthy taste, and there's no caffeine in it.

Make it easyCooked beets come in vacuum packs by the salad kits. One pack is about four of these.

Drink 02Juices and shots

Beet Orange Ginger Sunrise

Orange and ginger on top, beet at the bottom. Don't stir it till you're ready to drink it.

5 minTo make
20 to 30 minBefore you train
1 tall glassMakes

What goes in

  • 1 orange, peeled
  • ½ tsp grated fresh ginger
  • ½ cup cold water
  • ¼ cup cooked beet, chopped
  • 2 tbsp water for the beet
  • Ice

Make it

01Blend the orange, ginger and ½ cup water until smooth.
02Rinse the blender and blend the beet with 2 tbsp water.
03Fill a tall glass with ice, pour in the orange, then pour the beet slowly down the side so it sinks.

In the glassA whole orange in the glass, pith and all, with the ginger's bite on top.

Make it easyGrate the ginger on the small holes of a cheese grater, skin and all. It keeps in the freezer for months.

Drink 03Juices and shots

Orange Carrot Zinger

It's bright orange and sweet, and the ginger and lemon make it sharp.

5 minTo make
20 to 30 minBefore you train
1 tall glassMakes

What goes in

  • 1 orange, peeled
  • ½ cup carrot juice
  • ½ tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice (half a lemon)
  • ½ cup cold water
  • Ice

Make it

01Blend the orange, carrot juice, ginger, lemon juice and water for 30 seconds.
02Pour it over ice.

In the glassCarrot juice is naturally sweet, so the lemon and ginger keep it sharp.

Make it easyCarrot juice is in the refrigerated juice case. A whole carrot works too in a strong blender: chop it small and blend it longer.

Drink 04Juices and shots

Pink Raspberry Lemonade

Real raspberry lemonade. A spoon of beet makes it pink, and you can't taste it.

5 minTo make
20 to 30 minBefore you train
1 tall glassMakes

What goes in

  • ½ cup frozen raspberries
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice (1 lemon)
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 1 tbsp cooked beet
  • 1 cup cold water
  • Ice and a lemon wheel

Make it

01Blend everything except the ice for 30 seconds.
02Strain it through a sieve into a glass of ice to catch the seeds.
03Add the lemon wheel.

In the glassIt's lemonade with real fruit in it and one teaspoon of honey, a lot less sugar than the bottled kind.

Make it easySkip the strain if you don't mind the seeds.

Drink 05Coolers and spritzers

Watermelon Mint Cooler

It's blended watermelon and lime over ice with fresh mint, and it's the one to make when it's hot out.

5 minTo make
20 to 30 minBefore you train
1 tall glassMakes

What goes in

  • 2 cups seedless watermelon, cubed
  • 1 tbsp lime juice (half a lime)
  • 6 fresh mint leaves
  • Ice

Make it

01Blend the watermelon and lime juice for 20 seconds.
02Tear the mint into a tall glass, fill it with ice and pour the watermelon over it.
03Stir once and drink it cold.

In the glassWatermelon is about 90% water, so this is as much a glass of water as a drink.

Make it easyCube a whole watermelon on Sunday and keep it in a tub in the fridge. It's two minutes from there.

Drink 06Coolers and spritzers

Pomegranate Lime Spritzer

It's tart pomegranate, fresh lime and bubbles, with no blender and nothing to wash.

2 minTo make
20 to 30 minBefore you train
1 tall glassMakes

What goes in

  • ½ cup 100% pomegranate juice
  • 1 tbsp lime juice (half a lime)
  • ¾ cup sparkling water
  • Ice and a lime wheel

Make it

01Fill a tall glass with ice.
02Pour in the pomegranate juice and lime juice and stir.
03Top with the sparkling water and add the lime wheel.

In the glassThe sparkling water cuts the juice in half, so it's tart and light, not sweet.

Make it easyBuy the juice that says 100% pomegranate. The cocktail blends are mostly apple and grape.

Pineapple Ginger Fizz

Fresh pineapple and a kick of ginger, topped with bubbles.

5 minTo make
20 to 30 minBefore you train
1 tall glassMakes

What goes in

  • ½ cup fresh pineapple chunks
  • ½ tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tbsp lime juice (half a lime)
  • ¾ cup sparkling water
  • Ice

Make it

01Blend the pineapple, ginger and lime juice for 20 seconds.
02Pour it into a glass of ice.
03Top with the sparkling water and stir once.

In the glassPineapple's sweet enough on its own, so there's nothing added.

Make it easyA tub of pineapple chunks from the produce aisle saves cutting a whole one.

Green Apple Spinach Cooler

It's bright green, tart and cold, and it tastes like green apple, not like a salad.

5 minTo make
20 to 30 minBefore you train
1 tall glassMakes

What goes in

  • 1 green apple, cored and chopped
  • 1 cup baby spinach
  • ½ cup cucumber, chopped
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice (half a lemon)
  • 1 cup cold water
  • Ice

Make it

01Blend everything except the ice for 45 seconds, until smooth.
02Strain it into a glass of ice if you'd like it thinner, or drink it as it is.

In the glassA cup of spinach goes in, and the apple and lemon are all you taste.

Make it easyLeave the apple's skin on. The blender takes care of it.

Drink 09Coolers and spritzers

Watermelon Basil Slushie

Frozen watermelon blended into a slush. It's a summer training day in a glass.

5 minTo make
20 to 30 minBefore you train
1 large glassMakes

What goes in

  • 2 cups watermelon, cubed and frozen
  • 4 fresh basil leaves
  • 1 tbsp lime juice (half a lime)
  • ¼ cup cold water

Make it

01Blend everything, pushing it down with the tamper or stopping to stir, until it's a thick slush.
02Add a splash more water only if the blender stalls.

In the glassIt's frozen fruit and nothing else, so it's cold, sweet and mostly water.

Make it easyFreeze watermelon cubes on a tray first, then bag them, so they don't freeze into one block.

Banana Oat Smoothie

The one that's close to a small meal, for when lunch was a long time ago.

5 minTo make
45 to 60 minBefore you train
1 large glassMakes

What goes in

  • 1 banana, fresh or frozen
  • ⅓ cup rolled oats
  • 1 cup milk, any kind
  • 1 tbsp peanut butter
  • A pinch of cinnamon

Make it

01Blend the oats on their own for 10 seconds, until they're powdery.
02Add everything else and blend for 45 seconds, until it's smooth and thick.
03Drink it 45 to 60 minutes before you train.

In the glassBanana and oats are easy carbs, and the peanut butter makes it last.

Make it easyPeel and freeze ripe bananas in halves. A frozen one makes it thick and cold with no ice.

Cherry Vanilla Smoothie

Thick and creamy, like a cherry milkshake made with yogurt.

5 minTo make
45 to 60 minBefore you train
1 large glassMakes

What goes in

  • 1 cup frozen dark sweet cherries
  • ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
  • ½ cup milk, any kind
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract

Make it

01Blend everything for 45 seconds, until it's smooth.
02Add a splash more milk if it's too thick to pour.

In the glassThe Greek yogurt makes it thick and adds protein, and the frozen cherries mean there's no ice to water it down.

Make it easyFrozen cherries come pitted, so there's nothing to prep.

Berry Kale Smoothie

Deep purple and thick. The berries cover the kale completely.

5 minTo make
45 to 60 minBefore you train
1 large glassMakes

What goes in

  • 1 cup frozen mixed berries
  • 1 cup kale, stems out
  • ½ banana
  • 1 cup water or milk

Make it

01Blend the kale with the water or milk first, until there are no flecks.
02Then add the berries and the banana and blend until it's smooth.

In the glassA cup of kale goes in, and it tastes like berries.

Make it easyBagged chopped kale works. Pick out the thick stems.
