GLP-1 Recipe Guide Leads

    Call them

    They probably won't pick up, so leave this voicemail:

    Hey [Name], it's [Your Name]!

    I saw you requested our free GLP-1 recipe guide. I'm going to text you the link now so you have it handy and can check out all the recipes.

    Also, since you're looking for ways to support your daily nutrition while taking a GLP-1, I can send you some info on a supplement I've been really excited about lately. It's designed to help support daily nutrient and essential wellness needs while you're on a GLP-1.

    If you're curious about it, just reply back to my text and I'll send you the details. No pressure at all!

    If you don't see my text, check your Unknown Senders or Spam folder. On an iPhone, look for the filter or menu icon in the top right corner of your Messages app. On an Android, look for the three-dot menu. You should be able to find any filtered messages there, which is where my text might be.

    Okay, that's all for now! Let me know if you got the text. Talk soon!

  2. 2

    Text them

    As soon as you hang up, send this:

    Hey [Name], it's [Your Name]! 👋 Just left you a quick voicemail. Here's the link to the GLP-1 recipe guide you requested so you have it handy:

    https://assets.cdn.filesafe.space/FARA5f6fZAfOIsNbABzr/media/6a592594524a3ec4c63958b7.pdf

    Also, quick question - are you doing anything to make sure you're still getting the nutrients your body needs while taking a GLP-1? I've been sharing something with people that makes supporting those daily nutrition needs really simple.

    If you're curious, just reply INFO and I'll send you the info!

  3. 3

    Follow up in 24 hrs

    If they don't respond within 24 hours, text again:

    [Name]! Just following up on the above! 👆 Were you able to get the recipe guide? Want some info on the supplement I mentioned that helps support daily nutrient and wellness needs while on a GLP-1? Shoot me a text back - I can hook you up!

Tip: Personalize [Name] and [Your Name] every time. The recipe guide link is already in the text script - just hit Copy script and send it as-is.
