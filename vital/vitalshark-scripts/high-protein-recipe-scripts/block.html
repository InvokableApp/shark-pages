How to follow up with your High Protein Recipe Guide leads

Use these scripts immediately after receiving a new recipe guide lead.

High Protein Recipe Guide Leads
Watch first 1

Watch this before you start calling.

Do this, in order 3
  1. 1

    Call them

    They probably won't pick up, so leave this voicemail:

    Hey [Name], it's [Your Name]!

    I saw you requested our free high protein recipe guide. I'm going to text you the link now so you have it handy and can check out all the recipes.

    Also, since you're interested in adding more protein and making healthier choices, I can send you some info on a line of wellness supplements I've been really excited about lately. There are a variety of options designed to support different health and wellness goals.

    If you're curious about it, just reply back to my text and I'll send you the details. No pressure at all!

    If you don't see my text, check your Unknown Senders or Spam folder. On an iPhone, look for the filter or menu icon in the top right corner of your Messages app. On an Android, look for the three-dot menu. You should be able to find any filtered messages there, which is where my text might be.

    Okay, that's all for now! Let me know if you got the text. Talk soon!

  2. 2

    Text them

    As soon as you hang up, send this:

    Hey [Name], it's [Your Name]! 👋 Just left you a quick voicemail. Here's the link to the high protein recipe guide you requested so you have it handy:

    https://assets.cdn.filesafe.space/FARA5f6fZAfOIsNbABzr/media/20bb2bd3-5e64-446d-937f-ce3e757ce10b.pdf

    Also, since you're interested in adding more protein and making healthier choices, did you want to take a quick look at a line of wellness supplements I've been really excited about lately? There are a variety of options depending on what you're looking to support.

    If you're curious, just reply INFO and I'll send you the info!

  3. 3

    Follow up in 24 hrs

    If they don't respond within 24 hours, text again:

    [Name]! Just following up on the above! 👆 Were you able to get the recipe guide? Want some info on the wellness supplements I mentioned? Shoot me a text back - I can hook you up!

Tip: Personalize [Name] and [Your Name] every time. The recipe guide link is already in the text script - just hit Copy script and send it as-is.
