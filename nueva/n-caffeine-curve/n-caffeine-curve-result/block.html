Why bad sleep does not feel like caffeine The people in the study could not tell either.

Researchers gave twelve adults 400 mg of caffeine six hours before bed. They lost about an hour of total sleep a night.

The part worth sitting with: their own sleep diaries said they slept fine. The disruption only showed up on the equipment. At six hours out, self reporting missed it completely.

So this is not something you can catch by paying closer attention to how you feel. It does not feel like anything. It just quietly costs you.

Drake C, Roehrs T, Shambroom J, Roth T. Caffeine effects on sleep taken 0, 3, or 6 hours before going to bed. J Clin Sleep Med, 2013.

You are in a loop And the loop is the reason it never resolves.

  1. You sleep worse than you think you do.
  2. So the afternoon flattens you.
  3. So you reach for something at 3pm.
  4. Which is still in your blood at bedtime. Back to one.

Each turn causes the next one. That is why trying harder does not get you out of it: the crash is not a willpower failure, it is the loop doing exactly what it does.

The useful part is that it runs backwards just as well. Break one link and the whole thing turns around.
