Why breakfast

Only about 1 in 10 adults eat enough vegetables (CDC).

Breakfast is an easy place to add them. A handful of spinach or a cup of broccoli goes into eggs or oats without changing much else.

Every recipe here is still eggs, oats, toast, muffins or pancakes, with the greens cooked in.

Three things that make it easy

  1. Keep a bag of baby spinach next to the eggs. It goes from the bag to the pan with no chopping.
  2. Keep frozen peas, frozen chopped spinach and frozen broccoli in the freezer. They're already washed and cut, and they don't go slimy in the fridge.
  3. Cook once on Sunday. The frittata, the burritos, the muffins and the pancakes all keep, so weekday mornings are just reheating.

Pesto Scrambled Eggs on Toast

Soft scrambled eggs with pesto stirred through, on toast, with a pile of lemony arugula on top. It takes about 7 minutes.

  • Prep 2 min
  • Cook 5 min
  • Makes 1 toast each
IngredientServes 2Serves 4Serves 6+
Eggs4812
Basil pesto2 tbsp¼ cup6 tbsp
Butter1 tsp2 tsp1 tbsp
Bread (sourdough is good)2 slices4 slices6 slices
Arugula2 handfuls4 handfuls6 handfuls
Lemon½11½
Olive oila drizzlea drizzlea drizzle
Parmesan, shavedto tasteto tasteto taste

Method

  1. Put the bread in the toaster.
  2. Melt the butter in a pan over low heat. Whisk the eggs with a pinch of salt, pour them in and stir slowly for 3 to 4 minutes, until they're just set and still soft.
  3. Take the pan off the heat and stir in the pesto.
  4. Toss the arugula with a squeeze of lemon and a few drops of olive oil. Pile the eggs on the toast and the arugula on the eggs. Shave some parmesan over.

The good stuff

Pesto is mostly basil, and the arugula on top makes two kinds of greens on one plate.

Make it easy

A jar of pesto keeps a couple of weeks once it's open. Stir a handful of chopped spinach into the eggs too if you have one.

Savory Oats with a Soft Egg and Greens

Oats cooked in broth with kale stirred in, a soft egg and a spoon of chili crisp on top. If you've only ever had oatmeal sweet, try this once.

  • Prep 2 min
  • Cook 8 min
  • Makes 1 bowl each
IngredientServes 2Serves 4Serves 6+
Rolled oats1 cup2 cups3 cups
Chicken or vegetable broth2 cups4 cups6 cups
Kale, stems out, chopped (or spinach)2 cups4 cups6 cups
Parmesan, grated2 tbsp¼ cup6 tbsp
Eggs246
Soy sauce1 tsp2 tsp1 tbsp
Green onion, sliced123
Chili crisp or hot sauceto tasteto tasteto taste

Method

  1. Bring a small pot of water to a boil for the eggs. Lower them in and cook 6½ minutes, then move them to cold water.
  2. Meanwhile, simmer the oats in the broth for 3 minutes, stirring now and then.
  3. Stir in the kale and cook 2 minutes more, until it's soft. Take it off the heat and stir in the parmesan.
  4. Spoon into bowls. Peel and halve the eggs and set them on top, then add the soy sauce, green onion and chili crisp.

The good stuff

A big handful of kale per bowl, and the egg and parmesan make it a breakfast that lasts till lunch.

Make it easy

A fried egg works as well as a soft boiled one, and takes 3 minutes in the pan while the oats cook.

Spinach and Feta Breakfast Quesadilla

Scrambled eggs, wilted spinach, feta and mozzarella folded in a crisp tortilla. It takes about 10 minutes.

  • Prep 3 min
  • Cook 8 min
  • Makes 1 quesadilla each
IngredientServes 2Serves 4Serves 6+
Large flour tortillas246
Eggs369
Baby spinach2 cups4 cups6 cups
Feta, crumbled¼ cup½ cup¾ cup
Mozzarella, grated½ cup1 cup1½ cups
Butter or oil1 tsp2 tsp1 tbsp
Salsa, to serve

Method

  1. Wilt the spinach in a dry pan over medium heat, about 1 minute. Push it to one side.
  2. Scramble the eggs in the other side until just set, then stir the spinach in and slide it all onto a plate.
  3. Wipe the pan, add a little butter and lay in a tortilla. Cover half with mozzarella, then the eggs, then the feta. Fold it over.
  4. Cook 2 to 3 minutes a side, until golden and melted. Cut in half and serve with salsa.

The good stuff

Two cups of spinach for two people, and it shrinks so much you won't see most of it.

Make it easy

Keep the bag of spinach next to the eggs in the fridge. No chopping, no board to wash.

Smashed Pea and Feta Toast with a Jammy Egg

Frozen peas smashed with feta and lemon on toast, with a soft egg on top. It takes 12 minutes.

  • Prep 5 min
  • Cook 7 min
  • Makes 1 toast each
IngredientServes 2Serves 4Serves 6+

Eggs246

Method

  1. Lower the eggs into boiling water and cook 7 minutes. Add the peas to the same pot for the last 2 minutes.
  2. Lift the eggs into cold water and drain the peas.
  3. Mash the peas with a fork with the feta, lemon, olive oil, torn mint, salt and pepper. Leave it a bit chunky.
  4. Toast the bread, spread on the peas, and top with the halved eggs and a pinch of chili.

The good stuff

Half a cup of peas on each toast, which counts as a serving of vegetables.

Make it easy

Make a double batch of the smashed peas. It keeps 3 days in a jar in the fridge.

Turkish Eggs on Garlicky Spinach

Soft eggs on cool garlic yogurt and wilted spinach, with warm chili butter spooned over. Serve it with bread for scooping.

  • Cook 8 min

IngredientServes 2Serves 4Serves 6+

Method

  1. Stir the yogurt with the garlic and a pinch of salt, and spread it on the plates.
  2. Wilt the spinach in the oil with a pinch of salt, 1 to 2 minutes, and pile it on the yogurt.
  3. Poach the eggs for 3 minutes (or fry them) and set them on the spinach.

Make it easy

Broccoli Cheddar Frittata

Eggs, broccoli, spinach and cheddar baked in one pan. Bake it on Sunday and reheat a slice each morning.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Makes 1 frittata
IngredientServes 2Serves 4Serves 6+
Eggs4812
Milk2 tbsp¼ cup⅓ cup
Broccoli florets, chopped small1 cup2 cups3 cups
Baby spinach1 cup2 cups3 cups
Cheddar, grated⅓ cup⅔ cup1 cup
Green onions, sliced123
Olive oil1 tsp2 tsp1 tbsp
Salt and pepperto tasteto tasteto taste

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F.
  2. Warm the oil in an ovenproof skillet over medium heat. Add the broccoli and a splash of water and cook 4 minutes, until it's bright green and just tender.
  3. Add the spinach and stir until it wilts.
  4. Whisk the eggs with the milk, salt, pepper, the green onions and most of the cheddar. Pour it over the vegetables and leave it 2 minutes, until the edges set.
  5. Scatter the rest of the cheddar on top and bake 10 to 14 minutes, until the middle no longer wobbles. For Serves 6+, use a greased 9x13 dish and bake 20 to 25 minutes.

The good stuff

About a cup of broccoli and spinach goes into each serving, and the eggs make it filling.

Make it easy

Pour the same mix into a greased 12 cup muffin tin and bake at 350°F for 18 to 20 minutes. The egg muffins keep 4 days in the fridge or 2 months in the freezer. Microwave one for 30 to 45 seconds.

Zucchini Bread Overnight Barley

Overnight oats, made with rolled barley flakes and grated zucchini. It tastes like zucchini bread and you make it the night before.

  • Prep 5 min
  • Cook none, it soaks overnight
  • Makes 1 jar each
IngredientServes 2Serves 4Serves 6+
Rolled barley flakes1 cup2 cups3 cups
Milk, any kind1 cup2 cups3 cups
Plain Greek yogurt½ cup1 cup1½ cups
Zucchini, finely grated and squeezed⅔ cup1⅓ cups2 cups
Maple syrup4 tsp2½ tbsp¼ cup
Chia seeds2 tsp4 tsp2 tbsp
Cinnamon1 tsp2 tsp1 tbsp
Raisins2 tbsp¼ cup6 tbsp
Walnuts, chopped2 tbsp¼ cup6 tbsp

Method

  1. Grate the zucchini on the fine side of the grater, then squeeze it in a clean towel to get the water out.
  2. Stir everything except the walnuts together and spoon it into jars.
  3. Put the lids on and leave them in the fridge overnight, at least 8 hours.
  4. In the morning, stir. Add a splash of milk if it's thicker than you like, and top with the walnuts.

The good stuff

The zucchini disappears into it, and the barley flakes are a whole grain with plenty of fiber.

Make it easy

Barley flakes sit next to the oats or in the bulk bins. Pearl barley won't soften overnight, it has to be cooked first. The jars keep 3 days, so make three at once.

Zucchini Banana Muffins

Soft banana muffins with a cup of zucchini in the batch. Bake them once and grab one from the freezer all week.

  2. Grate the zucchini and press it lightly in a towel. It should be damp, not dripping.
  3. Mix the banana, eggs, maple syrup, butter, yogurt and vanilla, then stir in the zucchini.

Freezer Breakfast Burritos with Salsa Verde

Eggs, spinach, black beans and cheese rolled up with salsa verde. Make eight on Sunday, freeze them, and microwave one in two minutes.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 15 min
  • Makes 8 burritos
Ingredient4 burritos8 burritos12 burritos
Large flour tortillas4812
Eggs51015
Frozen chopped spinach, thawed and squeezed dry5 oz10 oz15 oz
Black beans, drained½ can1 can (15 oz)1½ cans
Cheddar or Monterey Jack, grated¾ cup1½ cups2¼ cups
Salsa verde¼ cup½ cup¾ cup
Green onions, sliced246
Ground cumin½ tsp1 tsp1½ tsp
Oil½ tbsp1 tbsp1½ tbsp

Method

  1. Squeeze the thawed spinach in a towel until it's really dry. Wet spinach makes a soggy burrito.
  2. Scramble the eggs with the cumin and a pinch of salt in the oil, and take them off the heat while they're still a little soft. Let them cool.
  3. Mix the spinach, beans and green onions.
  4. Lay out the tortillas. Down the middle of each: eggs, the spinach and bean mix, cheese and a tablespoon of salsa verde. Fold in the sides and roll them up tight.
  5. Let them cool completely, wrap each in parchment, then foil, and freeze in a bag. They keep 3 months.

The good stuff

Each one has spinach and black beans in it as well as the eggs.

Make it easy

To reheat, take the foil off, keep the parchment on and microwave 2 to 2½ minutes, turning it once. Or bake it in its foil at 375°F for 25 to 30 minutes from frozen.

Green Pancakes

Fluffy banana pancakes with a cup of spinach blended into the batter. They come out bright green, and the banana covers the taste of the spinach.

  • Prep 5 min
  • Cook 15 min
  • Makes about 8 small
IngredientServes 2Serves 4Serves 6+

Vanilla½ tsp1 tsp1½ tsp
Flour1 cup2 cups3 cups
Baking powder2 tsp4 tsp2 tbsp
Salta pinch¼ tsp½ tsp

Method

  1. Blend the spinach, milk, egg, banana and vanilla until it's completely smooth, with no green flecks.
  2. Whisk the flour, baking powder and salt in a bowl. Pour in the green mix and stir until just combined. A few lumps are fine.
  3. Heat a pan over medium low with a little butter. Pour in quarter cups of batter and cook about 2 minutes, until bubbles show, then flip and cook 1 to 2 minutes more.

The good stuff

Make it easy

Freeze the cooked pancakes flat in a bag and put them straight in the toaster from frozen.

Green Shakshuka with Feta

Eggs baked in a pan of garlicky spinach, leeks and peas, with feta and dill on top. It's one pan and takes half an hour.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Makes 1 pan
IngredientServes 2Serves 4Serves 6+
Olive oil1 tbsp2 tbsp3 tbsp
Leek, sliced thin (or ½ onion)1 small2 small3 small
Garlic cloves, sliced246
Ground cumin½ tsp1 tsp1½ tsp
Baby spinach5 oz bag10 oz15 oz
Frozen peas½ cup1 cup1½ cups
Lemon½11½
Eggs4812
Feta, crumbled¼ cup½ cup¾ cup
Dill or parsley, chopped2 tbsp¼ cup6 tbsp
Chili flakes, salta pincha pincha pinch

Method

  1. Warm the oil in a wide pan over medium heat. Cook the leek 5 minutes, until soft.
  2. Add the garlic and cumin and cook 1 minute.
  3. Add the spinach a handful at a time, stirring until each one wilts. Stir in the peas, a quarter cup of water, the lemon juice and a pinch of salt.
  4. Make little wells with a spoon and crack an egg into each. Cover and cook on low 6 to 8 minutes, until the whites are set and the yolks still soft.
  5. Top with the feta, the herbs and the chili. Serve it from the pan with toast or warm pita. For Serves 6+, use two pans.

The good stuff

A whole 5 oz bag of spinach cooks down to fit under four eggs.

Make it easy

Frozen chopped spinach works too. Thaw it and squeeze it dry, about 5 oz frozen for every bag of fresh.

Kale and Sweet Potato Hash

Crispy sweet potato, red onion and kale in one pan, with the eggs cooked right in it.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Makes 1 pan
IngredientServes 2Serves 4Serves 6+
Sweet potato, in ½ inch cubes1 medium2 medium3 medium
Olive oil1½ tbsp3 tbsp4 tbsp
Red onion, chopped½11½

Garlic clove, minced123
Smoked paprika½ tsp1 tsp1½ tsp
Eggs2 to 44 to 86 to 12
Salt and pepperto tasteto tasteto taste
Cooked breakfast sausage, sliced (optional)246

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large pan over medium. Add the sweet potato and a pinch of salt, cover, and cook 8 to 10 minutes, stirring a couple of times, until tender.
  2. Take the lid off, add the onion (and the sausage, if using) and cook 4 minutes, until the edges brown.
  3. Stir in the garlic and paprika, then the kale with a splash of water. Cook 2 minutes, until it wilts.
  4. Make wells, crack in the eggs, cover and cook 4 to 5 minutes, until the whites are set.

The good stuff

Three cups of kale for two people. It cooks down into the potatoes and picks up the smoky paprika.

Make it easy

Make the hash without the eggs on Sunday. It keeps 4 days. Reheat a portion in a pan and fry an egg on top.
