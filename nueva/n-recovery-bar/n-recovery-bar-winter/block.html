Recovery Nog

Eggnog without the eggs: banana, coconut milk and nutmeg.

760mg potassium
250mg sodium
75mg magnesium

You need

  • ¾ cup 2% milk
  • ¼ cup canned coconut milk
  • 1 small banana, sliced and frozen
  • ¼ tsp ground nutmeg
  • ¼ tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp maple syrup
  • a dash of salt

Make it

  1. Blend the milk, coconut milk, frozen banana, nutmeg, cinnamon, maple syrup and a dash of salt until smooth.
  2. Grate a little more nutmeg on top.

760 mg of potassium and 75 mg of magnesium, with the banana bringing the most of each.

Cranberry Orange Smash

Fresh cranberries and a whole orange, smashed, with a sugar and salt rim.

470mg potassium
35mg sodium
20mg magnesium

+ up to 290 mg sodium on the rim

You need

  • 1 orange, peeled
  • ¼ cup fresh cranberries
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • ½ cup coconut water
  • ½ cup sparkling water
  • ⅛ tsp salt, for the rim
  • 1 tsp sugar, for the rim
  • A few cranberries, to finish
  • Orange twist, to finish

Make it

  1. Mix the salt and sugar on a saucer. Run an orange wedge around half the rim and dip it.
  2. Muddle the orange segments, cranberries and honey in the glass until the cranberries split.
  3. Fill with ice, add the coconut water and top with sparkling water.

Most of the sodium is on the rim: 290 mg, next to 35 mg in the glass. The orange brings half of the 470 mg of potassium.

Salted Peppermint Hot Cocoa

For the class on a cold morning.

465mg potassium
275mg sodium
60mg magnesium

You need

  • 1 cup 2% milk
  • 1 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup
  • 4 fresh peppermint leaves
  • a dash of salt

Make it

  1. Warm the milk with the peppermint leaves until it steams. Don't let it boil.
  2. Take out the leaves and whisk in the cocoa, maple syrup and a dash of salt.

The cocoa and milk bring 60 mg of magnesium between them.

Clementine Rosemary Spritz

Two clementines and a sprig of rosemary, the smell of the holidays.

465mg potassium
190mg sodium
20mg magnesium

You need

  • 2 clementines, peeled
  • ½ cup coconut water
  • ½ cup sparkling water
  • a dash of salt
  • Rosemary sprig, to finish

Make it

  1. Muddle the peeled clementines in the bottom of a glass until they're juicy.
  2. Fill with ice. Add the coconut water and a dash of salt and stir.
  3. Top with sparkling water and tuck in the rosemary.

465 mg of potassium, more than half of it from the clementines.

New Year's Sparkler

Gold and fizzy in a champagne glass, for midnight.

310mg potassium
35mg sodium
20mg magnesium

+ up to 290 mg sodium on the rim

You need

  • ½ cup fresh pineapple chunks
  • ½ cup coconut water
  • 1 tbsp lime juice (half a lime)
  • ½ cup sparkling water
  • ⅛ tsp salt, for the rim
  • 1 tsp sugar, for the rim
  • Lime twist, to finish

Make it

  1. Mix the salt and sugar on a saucer. Wet half the rim of a champagne glass and dip it.
  2. Blend the pineapple, coconut water and lime juice until smooth and pour it in.
  3. Top with sparkling water.

310 mg of potassium from the coconut water and pineapple. The rim brings 290 mg of sodium.
