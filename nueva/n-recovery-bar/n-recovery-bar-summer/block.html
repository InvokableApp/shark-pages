Watermelon Mint Cooler

Watermelon with salt, made into a drink.

360mg potassium
295mg sodium
30mg magnesium

You need

  • 2 cups diced watermelon
  • 4 fresh mint leaves
  • 1 tbsp lime juice (half a lime)
  • ⅛ tsp salt
  • Watermelon wedge, to finish

Make it

  1. Blend the watermelon, mint, lime juice and salt until smooth.
  2. Pour over ice.

295 mg of sodium and 360 mg of potassium, nearly all of the potassium from the watermelon.

Piña Colada Slush

A piña colada slush, with no rum.

525mg potassium
195mg sodium
55mg magnesium

You need

  • 1 cup pineapple chunks, frozen
  • ¼ cup canned coconut milk
  • ½ cup coconut water
  • 1 tbsp lime juice (half a lime)
  • a dash of salt

Make it

  1. Blend the frozen pineapple, coconut milk, coconut water, lime juice and a dash of salt until thick.

525 mg of potassium and 55 mg of magnesium, the coconut milk bringing the most magnesium.

Berry Coconut Ice Pops

Six ice pops to keep in the freezer for after a hot run.

155mg potassium
65mg sodium
10mg magnesium

You need

  • 1 cup strawberries, halved
  • 1 cup blueberries
  • 1½ cups coconut water
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • ⅛ tsp salt

Make it

  1. Blend the strawberries, blueberries, coconut water, honey and salt until smooth.
  2. Pour into six ice pop molds and freeze for at least 6 hours.

Each pop has 155 mg of potassium, most of it from the coconut water.

Peach Lime Spritz

A ripe peach, blended and topped with bubbles.

505mg potassium
35mg sodium
20mg magnesium

+ up to 290 mg sodium on the rim

You need

  • 1 medium peach, sliced
  • 1 tbsp lime juice (half a lime)
  • ½ cup coconut water
  • ½ cup sparkling water
  • ⅛ tsp salt, for the rim
  • Peach slice, to finish

Make it

  1. Salt half the rim with ⅛ tsp salt.
  2. Blend the peach, lime juice and coconut water until smooth and pour it over ice.
  3. Top with sparkling water.

The peach brings over half of the 505 mg of potassium. The rim brings the sodium.

Cantaloupe Agua Fresca

The Mexican fruit water you see in big glass jars.

665mg potassium
200mg sodium
30mg magnesium

You need

  • 1½ cups cubed cantaloupe
  • ½ cup cold water
  • 1 tbsp lime juice (half a lime)
  • 1 tsp honey
  • a dash of salt
  • Lime wheel, to finish

Make it

  1. Blend the cantaloupe, water, lime juice, honey and a dash of salt until smooth.
  2. Pour over ice. Strain it if you like it thinner, though the numbers are for unstrained.

665 mg of potassium, almost all from the cantaloupe.
