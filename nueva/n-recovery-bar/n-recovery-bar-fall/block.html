Spiced Apple Smash

A whole apple blended with cinnamon, poured over ice.

620mg potassium
220mg sodium
25mg magnesium

You need

  • 1 apple, cored and chopped
  • 1 cup coconut water
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • ¼ tsp cinnamon
  • a dash of salt
  • Cinnamon stick, to finish
  • Apple slice, to finish

Make it

  1. Blend the apple, coconut water, lemon juice, cinnamon and a dash of salt until smooth.
  2. Pour over ice and stir with the cinnamon stick.

620 mg of potassium, two thirds from the coconut water and the rest from the apple.

Pumpkin Pie Smoothie

Pumpkin pie with the crust left out.

975mg potassium
280mg sodium
85mg magnesium

You need

  • ½ cup canned pumpkin
  • 1 small banana, sliced and frozen
  • 1 cup 2% milk
  • ½ tsp pumpkin pie spice
  • 1 tsp maple syrup
  • a dash of salt

Make it

  1. Blend the pumpkin, frozen banana, milk, pumpkin pie spice, maple syrup and a dash of salt until smooth.

975 mg of potassium and 85 mg of magnesium. Use plain canned pumpkin, the can that says 100% pumpkin.

Concord Grape Fizz

Grape soda, made with real Concord grapes.

395mg potassium
35mg sodium
15mg magnesium

+ up to 290 mg sodium on the rim

You need

  • 1 cup Concord grapes
  • ½ cup coconut water
  • 1 tbsp lime juice (half a lime)
  • ½ cup sparkling water
  • ⅛ tsp salt, for the rim
  • A few grapes, to finish

Make it

  1. Salt half the rim with ⅛ tsp salt.
  2. Muddle the grapes in the glass until they've all burst.
  3. Fill with ice, add the coconut water and lime juice and top with sparkling water.

The rim brings 290 mg of sodium. Coconut water and grapes bring 395 mg of potassium.

Make it easy: Concord grapes are in stores in September and October. Any dark grape works the rest of the year.

Maple Date Shake

Sweetened with two dates, thick like a milkshake.

695mg potassium
270mg sodium
55mg magnesium

You need

  • 2 Medjool dates, pitted
  • 1 cup 2% milk
  • 1 tsp maple syrup
  • ¼ tsp cinnamon
  • a dash of salt

Make it

  1. Soak the dates in hot water for 5 minutes if they're firm.
  2. Blend the dates, milk, maple syrup, cinnamon, a dash of salt and a handful of ice until smooth.

695 mg of potassium, half from the milk and half from the dates.
