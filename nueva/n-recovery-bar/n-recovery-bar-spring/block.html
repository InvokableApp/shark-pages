Strawberry Basil Smash

Strawberries and basil, muddled and topped with fizz.

345mg potassium
190mg sodium
20mg magnesium

You need

  • ½ cup strawberries, halved
  • 4 basil leaves
  • 1 tbsp lime juice (half a lime)
  • 1 tsp honey
  • ½ cup coconut water
  • ½ cup sparkling water
  • a dash of salt
  • Basil leaf, to finish

Make it

  1. Muddle the strawberries, basil, lime juice and honey in the bottom of a glass.
  2. Fill with ice. Add the coconut water and a dash of salt and stir.
  3. Top with sparkling water.

345 mg of potassium, mostly the coconut water and strawberries.

Cucumber Lime Cooler

The coldest-tasting drink in here.

645mg potassium
360mg sodium
35mg magnesium

You need

  • ½ cucumber, chopped
  • 1 cup coconut water
  • 1 tbsp lime juice (half a lime)
  • 1 tsp honey
  • ⅛ tsp salt
  • Cucumber ribbon, to finish

Make it

  1. Blend the cucumber, coconut water, lime juice, honey and salt until smooth.
  2. Pour over ice.

360 mg of sodium and 645 mg of potassium.

Apricot Honey Spritz

Three ripe apricots, blended and topped with bubbles.

495mg potassium
35mg sodium
20mg magnesium

+ up to 290 mg sodium on the rim

You need

  • 3 ripe apricots, pitted
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp honey
  • ½ cup coconut water
  • ½ cup sparkling water
  • ⅛ tsp salt, for the rim
  • Apricot slice, to finish

Make it

  1. Salt half the rim: ⅛ tsp salt on a saucer, a lemon wedge around the rim, then dip.
  2. Blend the apricots, lemon juice, honey and coconut water until smooth and pour over ice.
  3. Top with sparkling water.

The apricots bring over half of the 495 mg of potassium. The rim brings 290 mg of sodium.

Carrot Orange Smoothie

Carrot and orange, smoothed out with a frozen banana.

975mg potassium
225mg sodium
55mg magnesium

You need

  • ½ cup grated carrot
  • 1 orange, peeled
  • 1 small banana, sliced and frozen
  • ½ cup coconut water
  • a dash of salt

Make it

  1. Blend the grated carrot, peeled orange, frozen banana, coconut water and a dash of salt until completely smooth.

975 mg of potassium from the banana, orange, carrot and coconut water.

Make it easy: Grate the carrot first, even in a strong blender, or it stays gritty.
