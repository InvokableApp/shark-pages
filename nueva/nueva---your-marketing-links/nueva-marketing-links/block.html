    Somewhere right now, someone is typing out the exact problem you solve, in public, and asking strangers for help. Your job is to find those people and be the most useful answer in the thread.

    Do not stop at Facebook. REDDIT, NEXTDOOR, and QUORA are full of the same conversations, and most people in your industry are not showing up in any of them. Search your topic, sort by recent, and look for the posts that end in a question mark.
