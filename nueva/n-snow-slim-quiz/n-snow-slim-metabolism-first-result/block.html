The ninety days that cost nothing

Nothing here is about eating less. You have already done that, and it is the lever that stopped working.

  1. Day 1, then monthly

    Use a tape, not the scale

    Waist at the navel, hips at the widest, mid thigh, upper arm. The scale cannot tell you where anything went, which is exactly the question you came here with.

  2. Week 1

    Stop cutting, and eat at maintenance for four weeks

    A long stretch under maintenance is the most common reason a body stops reshaping. Eating at maintenance feels like doing nothing, which is exactly why almost nobody tries it, and it is the single change most likely to be missing from your last two years.

  3. Most days

    Protein at the first meal, not just the last

    Most people who train get their protein in at dinner and almost none at breakfast. Spreading it changes what the day is built from without changing the total.

  4. 3 times a week

    Heavier, for fewer reps

    If your training has been mostly circuits, classes and high reps, the missing stimulus is load. Fewer reps, more weight, longer rests. It is a different signal, not more work.

  5. Every day

    Ten thousand steps that are not a workout

    Walking that is separate from training is the part of daily output that quietly disappeared when your training got serious. It is also the part that does not make you hungrier.

  6. Weeks 1 to 4

    Seven hours, protected

    Short sleep changes what the body does with what you eat, and it is the input most likely to be short in someone who is fitting training into a full week.

  7. Day 1, then day 90

    Take the photo today

    Same light, same time of day, same clothes, from the front and the side. You cannot see a twelve week change in a mirror you look into every morning, and without the photo you will conclude nothing worked when something did.

What not to spend money on

Being told what to skip is worth more than being told what to buy, and it is the part almost nobody puts on a page like this one.

  • Another cutYou are here because the last one did not touch these areas. A deeper one is the same instrument, pressed harder, and it costs you the training quality that would have helped.
  • Fat burners and thermogenicsStimulants make you feel like something is happening. What they reliably do is raise your heart rate and cost you the sleep that this lane runs on.
  • Waist trainers, wraps and anything that promises spot reductionYou cannot choose where your body loses fat. Anything sold on that promise is selling compression, which lasts as long as you wear it.
