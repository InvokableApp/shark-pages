The ninety days that cost nothing

The evening is not a discipline problem. It is usually a protein problem, a sleep problem or a boredom problem, and those have different answers.

  1. Week 1

    Work out which evening it actually is

    For one week write one word after dinner: hungry, tired, or bored. Nothing else. Most people find it is the same word five nights out of seven, and the three words have completely different fixes.

  2. Most days

    Thirty grams of protein before midday

    The evening is usually built earlier in the day than it feels. A front-loaded day arrives at eight o'clock in a different state, without you having decided anything at eight o'clock.

  3. Every day

    Fiber with the first two meals

    Beans, oats, berries, vegetables with lunch rather than only with dinner. This is the least interesting item on the list and the one most likely to change the evening.

  4. Evenings

    Decide the evening at six, not at nine

    Put the thing you are going to have out on the counter early. A decision made while you are not yet tired is a different decision, and it is the whole trick.

  5. Weeks 1 to 4

    Two alcohol free nights a week

    Not zero. Two. Alcohol lowers what you decide to eat afterwards and costs the sleep that makes the next evening easier, and two nights is a change people actually keep.

  6. Weeks 2 to 12

    Keep the food you like in the week

    Plans that remove everything enjoyable get abandoned in week six, and you have a drawer of those. Build the evening you want to repeat, not the one you want to survive.

  7. Day 1, then day 90

    Take the photo today

    Same light, same time of day, same clothes, from the front and the side. You cannot see a twelve week change in a mirror you look into every morning, and without the photo you will conclude nothing worked when something did.

What not to spend money on

Being told what to skip is worth more than being told what to buy, and it is the part almost nobody puts on a page like this one.

  • Appetite suppressant teas and gummiesThe ones that do anything at all do it with caffeine or a laxative. You end up short on sleep or short on water, and both make tomorrow evening worse.
  • Meal replacement shakes as the fixThey work by removing the decision, which is why the evening comes back the moment you stop. Fine as a convenience, useless as an answer.
  • Buying an entirely new set of foodThe clear-out-the-cupboard reset is the most reliably abandoned move in the whole category. Change two meals, not your kitchen.
