The ninety days that cost nothing

This lane is slow and it is mostly built, not lost. Two of the seven below are the whole thing and the rest support them.

  1. Day 1, then monthly

    Use a tape, not the scale

    Waist at the navel, hips at the widest, mid thigh, upper arm. The scale cannot tell you where anything went, which is exactly the question you came here with.

  2. 3 times a week

    Resistance training, and progress the load

    This is the lane. Firmness is largely what is underneath, and the only reliable way to add to it is to train against a resistance that goes up over time. Classes and circuits maintain. They do not build.

  3. Every day

    Protein at every meal, not just after training

    Building anything needs the raw material available all day, not in one window. This is the most common gap in someone who trains well and eats reasonably.

  4. Week 1

    Stop cutting for a while

    You cannot build much of anything from a deficit. If the size is already where you want it, the deficit is now working against the thing you actually want.

  5. Every day

    Water, properly, and salt with it

    Well hydrated tissue looks noticeably different from tissue that is not, and this is the one item on the list that shows in weeks rather than months. It is also free.

  6. Weeks 1 to 12

    Seven hours, and be boring about it

    Almost all of the building happens asleep. Short sleep is the quietest way to train hard for twelve weeks and see very little for it.

  7. Day 1, then day 90

    Take the photo today

    Same light, same time of day, same clothes, from the front and the side. You cannot see a twelve week change in a mirror you look into every morning, and without the photo you will conclude nothing worked when something did.

What not to spend money on

Being told what to skip is worth more than being told what to buy, and it is the part almost nobody puts on a page like this one.

  • Firming creams, wraps and massagersThey move water around for a few hours. Nothing applied to the outside of an area changes what is underneath it, which is what you are actually looking at.
  • More cardioIt is the instinct, and here it is the wrong one. More cardio on top of a long cut takes from the exact tissue you are trying to build.
  • Another collagen powder on top of the one you haveOne is a reasonable thing to take. Two is money. Check what you already own before you add anything.
