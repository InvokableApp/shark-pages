Why it happens

The crash is what creates the craving

You're not losing an argument with yourself at 3pm. Your blood sugar climbs after lunch, insulin clears it and overshoots, and an hour or two later you dip below where you started. That dip is the tired, and it's the reason sugar suddenly sounds like the answer.

Willpower is being asked to fix something that was never a willpower problem, which is why trying harder hasn't worked. The plan flattens the climb instead.

Start here

The whole first week is one walk

Five to ten minutes after one meal a day, ideally lunch. Your muscles can pull glucose out of your blood without waiting for insulin, so a short walk while the food is still being absorbed takes the top off the climb. That's what makes the dip later on shallower.

Before you download it

Who this isn't for

This isn't a diet and it isn't a weight loss plan. Nothing in it changes what you weigh in two weeks, and it doesn't ask you to give anything up.

If your afternoons are hard because you aren't sleeping, or because of a medication, or because of something going on medically, a walk after lunch isn't the answer to that and we're not going to pretend otherwise.
