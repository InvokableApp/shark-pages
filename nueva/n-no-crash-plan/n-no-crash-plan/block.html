Why it happens

The crash is what creates the craving

You eat lunch and your blood sugar climbs. Insulin clears it and overshoots, so an hour or two later you're lower than you were before you ate. Low blood sugar feels like tiredness, and it makes you want sugar, because sugar is the fastest way back up.

So the craving at 3pm isn't a willpower problem. It's a dip that started at lunch, and that's why trying harder has never worked. Make the climb smaller and the dip gets smaller with it.

Start here

The whole first week is one walk

Five to ten minutes after one meal a day, ideally lunch. Working muscle pulls glucose out of your blood without waiting for insulin, so walking while the food is still being absorbed lowers the peak. A smaller peak means a smaller dip.

Before you download it

Who this isn't for

This isn't a diet and it isn't a weight loss plan. Nothing in it changes what you weigh in two weeks, and it doesn't ask you to give anything up.

If your afternoons are hard because you aren't sleeping, or because of a medication, or because of something going on medically, a walk after lunch isn't the answer to that and we're not going to pretend otherwise.

Day one is a ten minute walk

Do it after your next meal and day one is done. It's the move that does the most, so if you only ever change one thing, change this one.
