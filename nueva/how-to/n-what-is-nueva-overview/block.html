Send it like this

Short, no pitch, and it gives them an easy way out. The page does the explaining, so your message does not have to.

Hey! You asked what I have been doing. Easier to show you than explain it over text, so here is the two minute version. Have a look when you get a minute and tell me what you think.

After they have read it

Ask a question rather than making an offer. You are finding out whether there is real interest, not closing anything.

What did you think? Is it something you would want to try yourself, or does anyone come to mind who might?
