Quick Reminder Of How This Funnel Works

This funnel uses a DM / text-style confirmation page. Ideally, after someone opts in for your free guide or resource, they follow the instructions on the confirmation page and DM or text you to receive something additional…

That “something additional” might be:

  • Facebook group link
  • Product or sample information
  • Additional free resource
  • Coupon or discount code
  • Other offer related to the funnel

The moment a lead comes through your funnel, here’s what you want to do:

  1. 1

    Read which button they pressed

    This page has two doors and they are two completely different conversations. One person wants to know which product to buy. The other wants to know whether they could do what you do. Your notification says which one.

    Then check Messenger, and check your Message Requests folder, then the Spam tab inside it. Messages from people who are not already your friend land there and stay hidden.

    ⚠️ This funnel has no opt-in, so treat the message as the real signal, not the notification. There is no form anywhere on the page: the buttons open a panel that sends them straight to you. That means you may get a notification with no name on it, or no notification at all for someone who has never been in your contacts. Nobody arrives on a list, so nothing here can be chased later. If they message, answer. If they do not, there is nothing to follow up.

  2. 🛍️ If they asked about the PRODUCTS

    They just read a page with six products on it, which is exactly enough to freeze someone. Do not send the shop link first. Ask one question, get one answer, then send them to one product.

    Hey name! It’s {{custom_values.nueva_rep_first_name}} 👋

    What are you actually trying to sort out? Energy, hydration, sleep, the afternoon slump?

    Tell me that and I’ll point you at the one I’d start with. There are six on that page and picking by yourself is how people end up with a cupboard of half used tubs.

    Messenger

    Once they have told you, send the shop link. Your notification carries it, and it is your link, so the order is credited to you.

  3. 💼 If they asked about the OPPORTUNITY

    This is the stronger of the two. They read the sharing section and chose to ask about it, which almost nobody does idly. Ask what made them curious before you explain anything. The answer tells you whether this is about money, time, or being bored, and those are three different conversations.

    Hey name! It’s {{custom_values.nueva_rep_first_name}} 👋

    So you had a look at the sharing side. What made you curious about it?

    Happy to just tell you what it actually is day to day. No pitch, and I’ll tell you if I don’t think it’s for you.

    Messenger

    After they answer, send them the page that explains the whole thing. Your notification carries that link too. Send it after the question, not instead of it.

  4. This is a page you send to one person, not a funnel you run ads to. Your lead magnets collect email addresses and then work on their own for a fortnight. This one does nothing on its own, because there is nobody to send anything to. It is what you send when somebody asks “so what is it you actually do?”, and it answers both halves of that question so you do not have to type it out again.

    Put it in your bio. Put it in your links page. Send it to the person who asked at a party. Then let it do the explaining, and pick the conversation back up when they message you.

And when they say…

A page with products on one side and a business on the other produces a very specific set of questions. These are them.

“Which one should I start with?”

Never answer with a list. One product, one reason, and a way out if it does nothing. Most people start with energy or hydration, and there is a money back guarantee that holds even on an empty container, so there is very little for them to lose.

One thing at a time, otherwise you can’t tell what’s working.

If it’s the afternoon that’s the problem, start with the energy one. If you’re just never drinking enough water, hydration. Those are the two most people begin with.

And there’s a thirty day money back guarantee that stands even on an empty tub, so if it does nothing for you, you send it back.

“Can I take this with my medication?”

Say less here, not more. You are not a doctor and this answer is never yours to give, no matter how confident you feel or how well you know them. Hand them the label and send them to someone qualified. It costs you nothing and it is the only safe version.

Honestly I’m not the person to answer that one, and I’d be guessing if I tried.

Every ingredient is printed on the label, so screenshot it and ask your pharmacist or your doctor. They’ll tell you in about a minute and they’ll actually know.

Tell me what they say and we’ll go from there.

“Is this an MLM?”

Answer it in the first word, and answer it with your chest. No joke, no “fair question”, no softening: any of that reads as though you were hoping they would not ask. You are not admitting to something, so do not word it like a confession.

Yes it is. It’s a billion dollar industry and it’s worked well for me, which is why I’m in it.

The half people never hear about is that you can just be a customer, or just share the products and get paid on what your customers buy. There’s no requirement to build a team at all.

Which bit were you asking about, the products or the earning side?

“Do I have to recruit people?”

The honest answer is no, and it is the single most useful thing on that page: commission is paid on customer purchases with no recruiting required. Say it plainly, because almost everyone assumes the opposite.

No. You get paid on what your customers buy, and you can do only that forever if you want.

There is a team side, and some people go and build one, but it’s a separate decision you’d make later. Nothing about the first part depends on it.

Most people I know started as a customer, liked the stuff, and just started saying so.

YOUR POSITION: BE THEIR FRIEND!

Your goal isn’t to immediately PITCH. Your goal is to START A CONVERSATION.

Ask questions. Learn about them. Find out:

  • Why did they download the guide?
  • What are they struggling with?
  • What are they trying to accomplish?
  • What would they like help with?

From there, you can naturally introduce your products, services, community, or opportunity when it actually makes sense for what they’re trying to accomplish.

Connect first. Build the relationship. Then position what you offer as a potential solution.

FINAL RECAP:

Speed to lead is crucial

Reach out to these leads as soon as possible! The longer you wait, the colder the lead gets. You want to STRIKE when the iron is HOT!

Not everyone is going to immediately DM / reach out to you after opting into the funnel

More than likely, you’ll need to keep the conversation alive / moving forward by DM’ing the person.

The fortune is in the follow up

Stay consistent with your follow up. People get busy, messages get missed, make sure you stay top of mind!

Be the lead’s FRIEND

Dig into their problems / struggles, offer support and mention that your product / service / opportunity may be a potential solution.
