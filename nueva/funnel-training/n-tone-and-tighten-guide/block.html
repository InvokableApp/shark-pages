For the readers… The funnel explained in simple language 👇

This funnel begins with an opt-in page that collects your lead’s information, including their name, email address, phone number, and any other details requested on the form.

Once they submit the form, their information is added to your account, and they are redirected to a confirmation page.

The confirmation page has information on it (text and sometimes a video) that communicates two main things to your lead…

  1. Number 1:

    We tell your lead to check their email for the resource they just requested access to, making sure they know to check their spam folder.

  2. Number 2:

    We include messaging to your lead to do something else to help open up a conversation with YOU (like DM to get product info, or get an additional resource etc.)

The primary goal of this funnel:

To drive leads (build your list of potential customers / reps) and encourage your leads to message you.

Understand this: Not every lead will DM or text you immediately after opting into your funnel. To learn how to follow up with both those who contact you and those who don’t, access the training.

What to post for this funnel

Post CONSISTENTLY about the free guide. Stories 2-3 times a week. Static posts 1-2 times a week.

Two angles again, and here angle 1 is the stronger one, because you are training and training is visible.

Angle 1: you are running the eight sessions
  • Twenty seconds of one movement, mid session. “Session 3 of 8. Twenty minutes, no gym, no shots. DM me and I’ll send you the guide.”
  • The clock. Start and finish, twenty minutes apart, and whatever you got done in between.
  • The honest one: the part of the guide you disagreed with at first, and whether it held up once you actually tried it. Skepticism reads as real.
  • A recipe you actually made, on your own plate. Not a stock photo, yours.
  • Session 8. What changed, what did not, and that you are running the eight again. Ask who wants to do it with you.

Call to action: “DM me and I’ll send it over.” Make them ask. The ones who do are already leads.

Angle 2: share the guide as a resource
  • Post the line the whole guide turns on: you cannot choose where your body loses fat. Sit ups do not flatten a stomach and inner thigh machines do not slim thighs. Post it and let people argue with you.
  • Say the numbers: eight twenty minute sessions and twelve high protein recipes, free, and it asks for an email and nothing else.
  • Ask the question it answers: “You already train. So why doesn’t it show yet?”
  • A photo after a session with the link right in the caption.
  • Drop it where women talk about training, food, or getting back into it.

Call to action: link in the caption. There is nothing to buy and nothing to join, so there is no reason to gate it.

Their answer tells you where they are before you have recommended a single thing.

Quick Reminder Of How This Funnel Works

This funnel uses a DM / text-style confirmation page. Ideally, after someone opts in for your free guide or resource, they follow the instructions on the confirmation page and DM or text you to receive something additional…

That “something additional” might be:

  • Facebook group link
  • Product or sample information
  • Additional free resource
  • Coupon or discount code
  • Other offer related to the funnel

The moment a lead comes through your funnel, here’s what you want to do:

  1. 1

    Check to see if they DM’d or texted you!

    Check Facebook and Instagram, and check your Message Requests folder, then the Spam tab inside it. Messages from people who are not already your friend land there and stay hidden.

    Keep the first message light. You are not selling anything here and you do not need a clever opener. Say hi, offer to answer questions, and put two easy things in front of them: the companion guide and your group.

  2. ✅ If they DID message you

    Answer whatever they asked, then offer the two things. Pair it with a quick voice note if you can, it lands completely differently to typed text.

    Hey name! It’s {{custom_values.nueva_rep_first_name}}, the person behind the Tone and Tighten guide 👋

    Let me know if you have any questions about it, happy to help.

    Btw I’ve also got a companion guide on which supplements are actually worth the money, and I’m in a Facebook group where people share their wins and what’s working. Interested in either?

    Messenger or text
  3. ❌ If they DID NOT message you

    ⚠️ Nothing will remind you on this funnel. Unlike The No Crash Plan, there are no automatic check ins here. They get the guide and one email, and after that it is entirely you. Put a reminder in your phone.

    A few days after they downloaded it

    Hey name! It’s {{custom_values.nueva_rep_first_name}}, the person behind the Tone and Tighten guide 👋

    Just saying hi. Let me know if you have any questions.

    Btw I’ve got a companion guide if you want it, the one on which supplements are actually worth the money. I’m also in a Facebook group where we share our wins and techniques. Interested in either?

    First nudge

    Then stop. The last message is a sign off, not another nudge. Say it is the last one, say out loud that you are a real person and not an autoresponder, and hand them the group on the way out. People who were never going to reply still read this one, and a fair number join.

    name! Last message from me, promise 😄

    And it’s not automated by the way, I just genuinely like building a bit of community around the stuff I’m into.

    If you want in, here’s the group: https://{{custom_values.nueva_community_group}}

    People post what’s actually working for them. Put my name in the join question so I know it’s you.

    Last message

  4. When they go looking at the product page or your shop, you get a notification and it is the strongest signal this funnel gives you. Do not sell into it. Ask about the workouts first, then offer to stop them buying the wrong thing.

    Hey name! Saw you were having a look 👀

    Before you order anything, tell me what you’re trying to sort out and I’ll tell you if that’s even the right one. There are a few ways to buy it and one is better value than the others.

    They are on the shop

    Or, if you run a group, offer it instead of a product. It is the softer move and it keeps them near you.

    name! I run a little group where people post what’s actually working for them, want me to send you the link?

    It’s free and there’s no pitch in it: https://{{custom_values.nueva_community_group}}

    Put my name in the join question so I know it’s you.

    Group

  5. If they go quiet on you, bump it. Do not apologise for texting and do not ask whether they got the guide.

    name! Just bumping this up 👆

    Any questions on the guide? And did you want that companion guide or the group link?

    Bump

And when they say…

Nothing above covers these, and nothing in your system will warn you they are coming. They are the ones that decide whether you get a customer.

“Which supplement should I be taking?”

Do not guess and do not list everything. Send the companion guide. It exists so you are not the one making the claim.

Good question, and the honest answer is it depends what you’re actually trying to fix.

This is the one I’d read first: https://{{custom_values.nueva_tone_and_tighten_companion_pdf_url}}

It shows you how to read a label in about a minute and which stuff is worth the money. Tell me what you land on and I’ll say where I’d start.

“I already train though, why do I need this?”

This is not an objection, it is the exact person the guide was written for. Say so.

That’s who it’s for! It’s not a beginner thing.

The whole point of it is that you cannot choose where your body loses fat. Sit ups don’t flatten a stomach. So if you already train and it still isn’t showing, it’s usually what you’re pairing together and the food, not effort.

What’s the bit that’s bugging you most?

“Is this an MLM?”

Answer it flat, in the first sentence. No joke, no “fair question”, no softening. Any of that reads as though you were hoping they would not ask.

It’s a network marketing company. I get paid when someone buys through my link, like an affiliate deal.

That’s why the guide itself has nothing to buy in it. I’d rather you just use it.

If you ever want the supplement side I’ll send it over, and if you don’t that’s genuinely fine.

“What does it cost?”

Do not recite a price list. There are several ways to buy and one is better value, so the answer is an offer to help, not a number. And if you can afford it, offer to send them a sample. Nothing answers a price question faster than not having to spend anything to find out.

Depends what you go for, there’s a few options and one’s better value than the others.

Want me to just send you a sample first? Then you’re not guessing, and if it does nothing for you at least it cost you nothing.

YOUR POSITION: BE THEIR FRIEND!

Your goal isn’t to immediately PITCH. Your goal is to START A CONVERSATION.

Ask questions. Learn about them. Find out:

  • Why did they download the guide?
  • What are they struggling with?
  • What are they trying to accomplish?
  • What would they like help with?

From there, you can naturally introduce your products, services, community, or opportunity when it actually makes sense for what they’re trying to accomplish.

Connect first. Build the relationship. Then position what you offer as a potential solution.
