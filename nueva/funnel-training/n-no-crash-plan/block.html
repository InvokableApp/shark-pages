For the readers… The funnel explained in simple language 👇

This funnel begins with an opt-in page that collects your lead’s information, including their name, email address, phone number, and any other details requested on the form.

Once they submit the form, their information is added to your account, and they are redirected to a confirmation page.

The confirmation page has information on it (text and sometimes a video) that communicates two main things to your lead…

  1. Number 1:

    We tell your lead to check their email for the resource they just requested access to, making sure they know to check their spam folder.

  2. Number 2:

    We include messaging to your lead to do something else to help open up a conversation with YOU (like DM to get product info, or get an additional resource etc.)

The primary goal of this funnel:

To drive leads (build your list of potential customers / reps) and encourage your leads to message you.

Understand this: Not every lead will DM or text you immediately after opting into your funnel. To learn how to follow up with both those who contact you and those who don’t, access the training.

What to post for this funnel

Post CONSISTENTLY about the free 14 day plan. Stories 2-3 times a week. Static posts 1-2 times a week.

There are two ways to post this one, and the strongest reps run both. One shares the plan as a free resource. The other is you, doing it, out loud.

Angle 1: you are on the plan

This is the one that gets DMs. People do not want a PDF, they want the thing that is working for somebody they know.

  • A selfie on a walk, mid plan: “Day 4 of a little 14 day thing I picked up for the 3pm crash. Walking after lunch instead of pouring another coffee. It is actually working. DM me and I’ll send it to you.”
  • Your phone open to the plan at the exact moment it is telling you to do something. No caption needed beyond “day 6”.
  • What your afternoon looked like on day 1 versus day 10. Not a body photo, just the honest version: what you got done, whether you needed the second coffee.
  • The thing you were most skeptical about, and whether it held up. Skepticism reads as real.
  • Day 14. What you kept, what you dropped. Say you are running it again and ask who wants in.

Call to action: “DM me and I’ll send it over.” Make them ask. The ones who do are already leads.

Angle 2: share the plan as a resource

The plan carries no company name anywhere on it, so it posts like something useful instead of something you are selling. This is the only funnel you have that can go straight into a group.

  • A story photo of your own 3pm moment: the second coffee, the parking lot nap, the stare at the screen. Caption it “who else hits a wall at 3pm?” and let people answer.
  • A stories poll: “3pm. Second coffee, or push through?” Post the plan the next day to everyone who voted.
  • Screenshot a page out of the plan itself and say where you got it.
  • Share a win. Someone ran the 14 days and something changed. Stories and results are the proof people want, think Amazon reviews.
  • Drop it in a group where people talk about energy, sleep, focus or getting through a workday.

Call to action: put the link straight in the caption. It is free and it works for anybody, so there is no reason to gate it.

Either way, the DM that matters comes later

The plan itself tells them they can ask you for the second guide, What To Take. You get a text the moment they do. That text is your lead, because asking cost them something.

Send the guide and one question: “Here it is (name). Out of curiosity, what does your afternoon usually look like?”

Your check ins land on day 2, day 5 and day 11, with a group invite on day 13. They are about how the person is doing, never about the download.

Paid advertising can be an incredibly valuable way to generate leads, but it is important to begin with the right expectations.

Running ads will help you attract new leads and grow your email list. While the system will automatically send emails to help nurture those leads, your personal follow-up is what makes the biggest difference.

Take the time to connect with your leads, build genuine relationships, learn about their goals and challenges, and determine whether your products, services, or opportunity could be the right solution for them.

Paid ads do not always produce an immediate sale or sign-up. Their true value comes from keeping your pipeline full, growing your audience, and consistently giving you new people to follow up with.

Does working the leads require time and effort? Absolutely. However, that is part of the process. If you follow up consistently and build real connections, it can pay off in a BIG way.

We highly recommend using paid ads, but they should be used alongside the other lead-generation strategies outlined on this page.

Quick Reminder Of How This Funnel Works

This funnel uses a DM / text-style confirmation page. Ideally, after someone opts in for your free guide or resource, they follow the instructions on the confirmation page and DM or text you to receive something additional…

That “something additional” might be:

  • Facebook group link
  • Product or sample information
  • Additional free resource
  • Coupon or discount code
  • Other offer related to the funnel

The moment a lead comes through your funnel, here’s what you want to do:

  1. 1

    Check to see if they DM’d or texted you!

    The plan tells them they can ask you for the second guide, What To Take, so the ones who want it come to you. Check Facebook and Instagram, and check your Message Requests folder, then the Spam tab inside it. Messages from people who are not already your friend land there and stay hidden.

  2. ✅ If they DID message you

    Send the guide straight away. They asked, and asking cost them something, so speed matters more than polish here. Pair it with a quick voice note if you can, it lands completely differently to typed text.

    Hey name! It’s {{custom_values.nueva_rep_first_name}} 👋

    You asked what to take alongside the plan, so here you go: https://{{custom_values.nueva_no_crash_plan_supplement_pdf_url}}

    Fair warning, it’s short. It’s basically just what actually helps with the afternoon crash and what’s a waste of money.

    How’s it going so far? Are you doing the after lunch walk, or is that one not happening yet lol

    Messenger or text
  3. ❌ If they DID NOT message you

    Then it is on you to start the conversation. Your check ins fire automatically on day 2, day 5, day 11 and day 13, so you will be prompted. This is what to send when you are.

    One thing that matters more here than on any other funnel: the plan ships under Everyday Well and carries no Nueva mark anywhere. They have no idea who you are. Say so in the first line or it reads as a stranger texting out of nowhere.

    Day 2, just say hello

    Hey name! It’s {{custom_values.nueva_rep_first_name}}, the person behind The No Crash Plan 👋

    Just saying hi. Let me know if you have any questions.

    Btw I’m also in a Facebook group where we share our wins and what’s working, want me to send you the link?

    Day 2

    Day 5, if you have not heard back. Skip it if you spoke recently.

    name! Me again 😄

    How’s it going? Any questions I can answer?

    Day 5

  4. Once you know they have the plan, this is where it turns into a conversation about product. Day 11 is the natural moment, because they have either felt something change or they have not, and both are worth hearing.

    name! You’re near the end of the fourteen days, how has it gone?

    If you want the one on what to actually take and what to skip, say the word and I’ll send it: https://{{custom_values.nueva_no_crash_plan_supplement_pdf_url}}

    Day 11 · product

    Or, if you run a group, day 13 is the invite. This one is not a pitch, it is somewhere for them to ask questions between your messages.

    name! I run a little group where people post what’s working for them, want me to send you the link?

    It’s free and there’s no pitch in it: https://{{custom_values.nueva_community_group}}

    Put my name in the join question so I know it’s you.

    Day 13 · group

  5. If they go quiet on you, bump it. Do not apologise for texting and do not ask whether they got the guide.

    name! Just bumping this up 👆

    Any questions? And did you want that guide on what to take, or the group link?

    Bump

And when they say…

Nothing above covers these, and nothing in your system will warn you they are coming. They are the ones that decide whether you get a customer.

“Which one should I take?”

Do not guess and do not list everything. One question, then the guide, then one recommendation. The guide exists so you are not the one making the claim.

Good question. Quick one first: is it the afternoon crash you’re trying to fix, or more your energy across the whole day?

Either way, this is the one I’d read: https://{{custom_values.nueva_no_crash_plan_supplement_pdf_url}}

It says what’s actually worth the money and what isn’t. Tell me which way you lean and I’ll point you at where I’d start.

“Is this an MLM?”

Answer it flat, in the first sentence. No joke, no “fair question”, no softening. Any of that reads as though you were hoping they would not ask.

It’s a network marketing company. I get paid when someone buys through my link, like an affiliate deal.

That’s why the plan itself has nothing to buy in it. I’d rather you just use it.

If you ever want the supplement side I’ll send it over, and if you don’t that’s genuinely fine.

“What does it cost?”

Do not recite a price list. There are several ways to buy and one is better value, so the answer is an offer to help, not a number.

Depends what you go for, there’s a few options. Want me to send you the one I’d start with?

YOUR POSITION: BE THEIR FRIEND!

Your goal isn’t to immediately PITCH. Your goal is to START A CONVERSATION.

Ask questions. Learn about them. Find out:

  • Why did they download the guide?
  • What are they struggling with?
  • What are they trying to accomplish?
  • What would they like help with?

From there, you can naturally introduce your products, services, community, or opportunity when it actually makes sense for what they’re trying to accomplish.

Connect first. Build the relationship. Then position what you offer as a potential solution.

FINAL RECAP:

Speed to lead is crucial

Reach out to these leads as soon as possible! The longer you wait, the colder the lead gets. You want to STRIKE when the iron is HOT!

Not everyone is going to immediately DM / reach out to you after opting into the funnel

More than likely, you’ll need to keep the conversation alive / moving forward by DM’ing the person.

The fortune is in the follow up

Stay consistent with your follow up. People get busy, messages get missed, make sure you stay top of mind!

Be the lead’s FRIEND
