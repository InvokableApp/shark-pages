For the readers… The funnel explained in simple language 👇

This funnel begins with an opt-in page that collects your lead’s information, including their name, email address, phone number, and any other details requested on the form.

Once they submit the form, their information is added to your account, and they are redirected to a confirmation page.

The confirmation page has information on it (text and sometimes a video) that communicates two main things to your lead…

  1. Number 1:

    We tell your lead to check their email for the resource they just requested access to, making sure they know to check their spam folder.

  2. Number 2:

    We include messaging to your lead to do something else to help open up a conversation with YOU (like DM to get product info, or get an additional resource etc.)

The primary goal of this funnel:

To drive leads (build your list of potential customers / reps) and encourage your leads to message you.

What to post for this funnel

Post CONSISTENTLY about the quiz and about your own week. Stories 2-3 times a week. Static posts 1-2 times a week.

This is the only funnel you have that is about the business rather than a product, and it behaves differently. The quiz does not name Nueva until the last page, so it posts like a genuinely useful thing instead of a pitch. There are two ways to run it and the strongest reps run both.

Angle 1: share the quiz as a resource

The landing page asks what they want and what they can realistically give. No company, no product, no logo until they have already decided they are interested. That means it can go places a product post cannot.

  • A story with a poll: “If you could add $500 a month without quitting your job, would you actually do the work?” Post the quiz link the next day to everyone who voted yes.
  • The honest version of why you started looking. The car repair, the grocery bill, the year you did not take a holiday. Then: “I took a quiz that sorted out what would actually fit my week. Sixty seconds.”
  • “Most side hustles fail because they need hours you don’t have.” Then the quiz, which asks that question first.
  • A screenshot of one of the questions. The one about how many hours a week you have is the one people stop on, because it takes them seriously.
  • Drop it where people are already talking about money, second jobs, working from home, or leaving a job they are done with.

Call to action: put the link straight in the caption. Do not make them DM you for the quiz. It is a sixty second quiz, not a gated download, and the whole funnel is built to bring them back to you once they have taken it. Gating it just loses the ones who would have finished it at a red light.

Angle 2: you, doing it, out loud

This is the one that gets DMs. Nobody asks to see a compensation plan. They ask because it is working for somebody they actually know.

  • What your week actually looks like. Not “financial freedom”, the real thing: an hour after the kids are down, your phone at a football practice, forty minutes on a Sunday.
  • Your first commission, whatever it was. Small and real beats big and vague every time, and people believe the small one.
  • The part you were wrong about. You thought you would have to pitch friends, and here is what you actually do instead. Skepticism reads as real.
  • A photo of you doing something ordinary with the caption “this is the job”. The gap between what people imagine and what it is is the whole post.
  • Answer a question somebody actually asked you, out loud, in a story. Those are the best posts you will make and they cost nothing to write.

Call to action: “DM me and I’ll tell you what it actually involves.” Here the thing you are sharing is you, so making them ask is right. Add the quiz link underneath for the ones who are not ready to talk to a person yet.

The one who clicked your Nueva link has decided to look at the business.

Either way, open the contact before you reply. Quick Reminder Of How This Funnel Works

This funnel uses a DM / text-style confirmation page. Ideally, after someone opts in for your free guide or resource, they follow the instructions on the confirmation page and DM or text you to receive something additional…

That “something additional” might be:

  • Facebook group link
  • Product or sample information
  • Additional free resource
  • Coupon or discount code
  • Other offer related to the funnel

The moment a lead comes through your funnel, here’s what you want to do:

    Open the contact before you type anything

They answered eight questions before they ever reached you, and every answer is on the contact record: what they do for work, how many hours a week they have, whether they want money or time, what they are trying to earn, and whether they said they could invest to start.

    Read it first. You will never get a lead who has told you more about themselves than this one, and replying without looking wastes the only real advantage you have.

YOUR POSITION: BE THEIR FRIEND!

Your goal isn’t to immediately PITCH. Your goal is to START A CONVERSATION.

Ask questions. Learn about them. Find out:

  • Why did they download the guide?
  • What are they struggling with?
  • What are they trying to accomplish?
  • What would they like help with?

From there, you can naturally introduce your products, services, community, or opportunity when it actually makes sense for what they’re trying to accomplish.

Connect first. Build the relationship. Then position what you offer as a potential solution.
