Setup guide

Set up your alert bot: Telegram, an AI key, GitHub and Railway

Swipe through it on your phone, then sit down at your computer with the prompt. The AI does the walking, this is the map.

  • Browser only
  • No coding
  • About $5 a month
What you'll need

Four accounts and a notes app

  1. TelegramOn your phone. Free.
  2. OpenAI or AnthropicFor the line that says why it moved. Pennies a month.
  3. GitHubHolds your bot's code. Free.
  4. RailwayRuns it day and night. About $5 a month after a one-time trial credit.

Your notes app holds each key for a few minutes, until it goes into Railway.

Part 1 · Telegram

Make your bot and find your chat id

  1. Open @BotFatherSend /newbot and pick a name. It gives you a token: save it in your notes.
  2. Tap Start on your new botA bot can't message you until you do.
  3. Open one address in your browserThe AI gives it to you. It shows your chat id, a number. Save that too.

That address has your token in it. Don't share it or a screenshot of it.

Part 2 · Your AI key

One key, with a spending limit on it

  1. Make an OpenAI or Anthropic accountEither works. The AI tells you where the key is.
  2. Set a small monthly limitThe bot only asks when an alert fires, a handful of times a month.
  3. Save the key in your notesNever paste it into the chat with the AI.
Part 3 · GitHub

Two files in a private repository

  1. Make an account and turn on two-step loginThen a new repository, set to Private.
  2. Add file, Create new file: bot.pyPaste the code the AI writes for you and commit it.
  3. One more file: ProcfileOne line in it, worker: python3 bot.py. It tells the server how to start your bot.
btc-alert-bot / bot.pybtc-alert-bot / Procfile
Part 4 · Railway

Switch it on and watch the test alert land

  1. Sign up with GitHubThe AI goes through Railway's pricing page with you first.
  2. New Project, Deploy from GitHub repoPick your bot's repository.
  3. Add your keys in VariablesYour token, chat id and AI key, the coin you picked, and SEND_TEST_ON_START set to yes. The AI lists every one. Then delete them from your notes.
  4. Your test alert lands in TelegramCheck the logs show a price check every 5 minutes, then set SEND_TEST_ON_START to no.
Keep it safe

Your keys live in one place only

  1. Railway's Variables screenNever in the code, never on GitHub, never in the chat.
  2. Two-step login everywhereGitHub, Railway, Telegram and your AI account.
  3. A key leaks? Cancel itMake a new one and paste it into Railway. The AI shows you how for each service.

Websites move their buttons. When your screen doesn't match, tell the AI what you see and it works from that.
