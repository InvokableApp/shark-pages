The mission 1/3

Every product sold helps feed a child in need.

A healthier life should be possible for more people, not just the ones who can afford it. One More is a community built around aloe and hops flower research, with one goal behind it: helping malnourished children.

The mission 2/3

Where the giving goes: MannaRelief.

In 1999, Sam and Linda Caster brought an aloe-based nutritional extract to an orphanage in Romania. That became MannaRelief, which now sends its Hope Boost supplement to children in need. Product sales start the conversation, and giving back gives it a purpose.
