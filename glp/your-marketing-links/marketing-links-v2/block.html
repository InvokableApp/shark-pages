    Action Item 6

    IF YOU’RE SERIOUS ABOUT BUILDING THIS BUSINESS, YOU NEED TO DO MORE OUTREACH.

    It really is that simple.

    And one of the EASIEST things you can start doing TODAY is reaching out to people already in your phone. 📲

    🚨 YOUR DAILY ACTIONS 👇

    1. Action item 1: Text AT LEAST 2 people from your contacts. EVERY. SINGLE. DAY.
    2. Action item 2: Follow up with AT LEAST 2 people every day from previous conversations.

    That’s literally it.

    Now you might be thinking… “Okay, but what do I actually SAY?”

    Don’t worry, we’ve made that easy too. Below, you’ll find simple scripts you can use to start the conversation.

    Do you have to copy and paste it word-for-word? Absolutely not. Change it. Personalize it. Make it sound like YOU based on who you’re texting. Or, if you like the scripts exactly as written, send them exactly as written.

    Don’t get caught up trying to craft the “perfect” message. The goal is to START MORE CONVERSATIONS AND FOLLOW UP MORE.

    CONSISTENCY WINS.

    • 2 people a day = 14 people a week.
    • 14 people a week = 60+ people a month.

    Those conversations ADD UP. Do you think you could convert a couple of those folks into customers or reps?

    OF COURSE YOU CAN! And you’re going to get better and better as you do more of it!

    Read the directions below, send your first messages TODAY, and make this a DAILY habit for as long as you’re building this business.

    2 PEOPLE. EVERY DAY. NO EXCUSES. 🚀

    … or more! But start with 2.

    Read below and take action NOW! 👇

    Why two people a day works

    Do this today

    1. Open your phone contacts. You will quickly realize you have hundreds, maybe even thousands, of people in there.
    2. Pick two people to text, start with the people you know / somewhat know, first.
    3. Send them this, or something close to it. Swap the bracketed bits for real names.

    Your convo starter text…

    Hey name, it’s your name! Long time no talk. Hope you’re doing well!

    Random ask, and seriously, if you’re not interested you can tell me to kick rocks 😅

    I’m helping a company market their products. And no, I’m not asking you to buy or join anything haha. I’m simply reaching out to some friends on my phone to get their honest feedback and see if the products might be of interest to you, or even someone you know.

    The company put together a quick 2 minute video that explains everything, and I’d genuinely love to get your take on it.

    Would you be open to me sending it over? Absolutely no pressure. Even if it’s not for you and no one comes to mind, that’s 100% okay. Your honest feedback alone would be super helpful and appreciated!

    IF THEY TEXT YOU BACK WILLING TO CHECK OUT THE INFO…

    Send them this video page.

    If you have an iDecide presentation, feel free to send them that instead.

    After they watch it

    Wait for their response and let what they say guide the conversation. If they come back positive, something like “yeah, the products look pretty cool”, your job is to find out whether there is actual interest. Ask:

    Awesome! Is it something you’d personally be interested in trying, or does anyone come to mind who you think might be interested?

    If they say yes, you have a conversation. From there, move it forward based on how interested they are. You might send more product information, invite them to your results group, share a testimonial, offer a sample if you have one, or just ask what they are looking for and point them at the right next step.

    Do not overcomplicate this part. Have a real conversation, ask questions, listen, and guide them to the next logical step.

    IF THEY DON’T RESPOND… FOLLOW UP!

    If you have not heard back in about 24 hours, follow up. Do not assume they are not interested. People get busy, forget to reply, or have not watched the video yet.

    Hey name, no pressure at all, just following up on the above! If you haven’t had a chance to check out the video, totally understand, I know life gets crazy 🙂 Just let me know!

    The goal is not to pressure anyone. It is to keep the conversation moving until you get an answer either way.

    The key is consistency. Do not miss or skip a day.

    Social media algorithms reward accounts that post consistently. The more often you show up, the better your chances of increasing your organic reach, getting more views, catching people’s attention, and creating content that takes off.

    And remember, most people need to see your message multiple times before they decide to reach out, buy, or join.

    Keep showing up. Keep posting. That’s the ticket. It’s not complicated. Just do it!

    Curious to know WHAT to post to your FEEDS?

    Somewhere right now, someone is typing out the exact problem you solve, in public, and asking strangers for help. Your job is to find those people and be the most useful answer in the thread.

    Facebook groups are one of the BEST places to start. There are hundreds, if not thousands, of relevant groups you can tap into, but you need to approach them strategically. Be sure to watch the video in this section for the complete training.

    Do not stop at Facebook. REDDIT, NEXTDOOR, and QUORA are full of the same conversations, and most people in your industry are not showing up in any of them. Search your topic, sort by recent, and look for the posts that end in a question mark.

    Then actually answer it. Give away the useful thing for free, in the thread, where everyone can read it. No pitch, no link drop. Being the person who helped is what makes someone check your profile, and your profile is what turns them into a lead.

    As mentioned in the previous section, consistency is KEY. You have to keep swinging if you want to get a hit. Show up every day and answer at least ONE real question, wherever you find it.
