Affiliate Marketing Aptitude

Role specific 3 min 10 questions No experience needed

Summary of the assessment

This assessment looks at whether you already have what makes affiliate marketing work in the health space. Not sales experience. Things like whether people come to you for advice, and whether you have a health story other people can learn from.

It takes about three minutes and there are no wrong answers. At the end you get your profile and the path that fits it.

What it measures

  • Influence and trust. Whether people in your life already come to you before they make a decision.
  • Your health story. What you have lived through that someone else is living right now.
  • Conversation comfort. How easily you talk to people about something that worked for you.
  • Market timing. How close you already are to the conversations people are having about GLP-1s.
