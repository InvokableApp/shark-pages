The funnel explained in simple language:

This funnel begins with an opt-in page that collects your lead’s information, including their name, email address, phone number, and any other details requested on the form.

Once they submit the form, their information is added to your account, and they are redirected to a confirmation page.

The confirmation page has information on it (text and sometimes a video) that instructs your lead to do TWO main things…

  1. First thing we tell your lead to do on the confirmation page:

    Check their email for the guide (making sure the lead knows to check their spam or promotions folder)

    FYI

    Sometimes your emails will land in your leads spam folder - this happens more so when you just get started, because you are using a brand new email, it needs to warm up. Don’t worry though, leads usually see / check their spam (because we make them aware of this).

  2. Second thing we tell your lead to do on the confirmation page:

    Learn more about your company’s products and DM or text you to ask questions, explore possible discounts, or see if samples are available, maybe join a Facebook support or testimonial group, etc.

    FYI

    The reason we may have included messaging on the confirmation page of this funnel about sampling is because mentioning samples and discounts gives leads an additional and enticing reason to start a conversation with you.

Paid advertising can be an incredibly valuable way to generate leads, but it is important to begin with the right expectations.

Running ads will help you attract new leads and grow your email list. While the system will automatically send emails to help nurture those leads, your personal follow-up is what makes the biggest difference.

Take the time to connect with your leads, build genuine relationships, learn about their goals and challenges, and determine whether your products, services, or opportunity could be the right solution for them.

Paid ads do not always produce an immediate sale or sign-up. Their true value comes from keeping your pipeline full, growing your audience, and consistently giving you new people to follow up with.

Does working the leads require time and effort? Absolutely. However, that is part of the process. If you follow up consistently and build real connections, it can pay off in a BIG way.

We highly recommend using paid ads, but they should be used alongside the other lead-generation strategies outlined on this page.

When a new lead comes in, SPEED MATTERS. The faster you connect with them, the more likely you are to start a conversation while their interest is still fresh. This page walks you through exactly what to do.

Quick Reminder Of How This Funnel Works

This funnel uses a DM / text-style follow-up. Ideally, after someone opts in for your free guide or resource, they follow the instructions on the confirmation page and DM or text you to receive something additional…

That “something additional” might be:

  • Facebook group link
  • Product or sample information
  • Additional free resource
  • Coupon or discount code
  • Other offer related to the funnel

To view what is being pitched on this funnel’s confirmation page, view the confirmation page for this funnel. All clear? OK, keep reading below!

As soon as you’re notified that a new lead has opted in, you need to take action.

  1. 1

    STEP 1: Check For Their DM

    First, check Facebook or Instagram for a message from the lead. Ideally the lead was compelled enough to take action that was communicated on the confirmation page, and DM’s or TEXT’S you… So, check your messages - be sure to check your Message Requests folder too!

  2. If they DID message you

    Send them whatever was promised on the confirmation page. Whenever possible, pair your response with a quick voice or video message to make the interaction more personal.

  3. If they DID NOT message you

    It’s time for YOU to start the conversation. If you were already communicating with the person on Facebook or Instagram, simply follow up there.

    Hey name! It’s your name - I saw you requested the guide name. Were you able to access it okay?

    DM or text
  4. If they say yes

    NOW you can naturally move the conversation forward. Pair what you say to what you want the lead to ideally do next. You can modify these scripts HOWEVER you’d like. For example, if you’re not willing to offer a sample, don’t mention a sample, etc. You get the idea.

    Awesome! I hope you enjoy / get value from it! Not sure if you saw, but we also have some great products designed to help with XYZ. Would you be interested in learning more or potentially trying a sample?

    Option A / product

    Awesome! I hope you enjoy / get value from it! I also have a free support group around XYZ. Would you like me to send you the link to join? It’s totally free and an AWESOME community

    Option B / group
  5. If they don’t respond, FOLLOW UP!

    Hey name! Just following up on the message above 👆

    Bump

  6. The Key: Confirm FIRST. Transition SECOND.

    Don’t immediately jump into pitching them. First, confirm they received what they requested. Once they respond, transition into the next step you want them to take - whether that’s learning about a product, requesting a sample, joining your Facebook group, etc. Your funnel’s confirmation page will tell you exactly what that next step should be.

  7. 2

    STEP 2: If They Don’t Respond, FOLLOW UP!

    Don’t assume someone isn’t interested just because they didn’t respond to your first message. People get busy. Messages get buried. Notifications get missed. Follow up - and continue following up. Remember: THE FORTUNE IS IN THE FOLLOW-UP!

  8. What If The Lead Came From A Paid Ad?

    If the lead came from a paid ad - or anywhere else where you’re not already connected with them on Facebook or Instagram - follow the exact same process using TEXT MESSAGE instead of DM. You already have their phone number from the funnel, so reach out, confirm they received what they requested, start the conversation, and then transition into the appropriate next step.

YOUR POSITION: BE THEIR FRIEND!

Your goal isn’t to immediately PITCH. Your goal is to START A CONVERSATION.

Ask questions. Learn about them. Find out:

Why did they download the guide? What are they struggling with? What are they trying to accomplish? What would they like help with?

From there, you can naturally introduce your products, services, community, or opportunity when it actually makes sense for what they’re trying to accomplish.

Connect first. Build the relationship. Then position what you offer as a potential solution.

FINAL RECAP:

Speed to lead is crucial

Reach out to these leads as soon as possible! The longer you wait, the colder the lead gets. You want to STRIKE when the iron is HOT!

Not everyone is going to DM you

More than likely, you’ll need to keep the conversation alive / moving forward by DM’ing the person.

The fortune is in the follow up

Stay consistent with your follow up. People get busy, messages get missed, make sure you stay top of mind!

Be the lead’s FRIEND

Dig into their problems / struggles, offer support and mention that your product / service / opportunity may be a potential solution.
