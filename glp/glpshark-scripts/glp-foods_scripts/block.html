How to follow up with your GLP Recipe Guide leads

Use these scripts immediately after receiving a new recipe guide lead.

GLP Recipe Guide Leads
Watch first 1

Watch this before you start calling.

Do this, in order 3
  1. 1

    Call them

    They probably won't pick up, so leave this voicemail:

    Hey [Name], it's [Your Name]!

    I saw you requested our free recipe guide featuring foods that may support your body's natural GLP-1 release. I'm going to text you the link now so you have it handy.

    I can also send you information about some wellness products designed to support natural GLP-1 activity. If you're interested, just reply back to my text. No pressure at all.

    If you don't see my text, check your Unknown Senders or Spam folder. On an iPhone, look for the filter or menu icon in the top right corner of your Messages app, its usually three little lines. On an Android, look for the three-dot menu. You should be able to find any filtered messages there which is where my text to you might be.

    Okay, that's all for now! Let me know if you received the text. Talk soon!

  2. 2

    Text them

    As soon as you hang up, send this:

    Hey [Name], it's [Your Name]! 👋 Just left you a quick voicemail - Here is the link to recipe guide you requested so you have it handy - https://assets.cdn.filesafe.space/hKYM2WhsSBKHjvCPGkZo/media/ba1e0bb7-ed2c-4a12-b7db-78ba4f72b199.pdf

    Also - did you want to take a free look at the all natural liquid drops and powder designed to support your body's natural GLP-1, GLP-2 & GIP activity? These products are pretty awesome - Just reply INFO and I'll hook you up.

  3. 3

    Follow up in 24 hrs

    If they don't respond within 24 hours, text again:

    [Name]! Just following up on the above! 👆 Were you able to get the recipe guide? Want some info on the natural GLP-1 support products? Shoot me a text back - I can hook you up!

Tip: Personalize [Name] and [Your Name] every time. The recipe guide link is already in the text script - just hit Copy script and send it as-is.
