Use these scripts the same day a sample request comes in.

Watch this before you start calling.

Where did this lead come from?

Do this, in order 2
  1. 1

    Reply and send them the link

    Answer where they reached out, in the message thread or right under their comment, while it is still open:

    Hey [Name]! 👋 Happy to send you a free 3 day sample.

    Just fill out the form here and I'll get it in the mail: [Your funnel link]

    It only asks where to send it, takes a few seconds.

  2. 2

    If they don't fill it in

    Give it a day. People mean to and then their phone rings:

    Hey [Name], did you get a chance to fill in the form? Takes about a minute and then I can get your sample out.

    If it's easier, just send me the address here and I'll do it for you.

Do this, in order 4
  1. 1

    Call them

    They probably won't pick up, so leave this voicemail:

    Hey [Name], it's [Your Name]!

    I saw you just requested a free 3 day sample of IGNYT, and I'm the one getting it in the mail to you.

    I only need one thing before it goes out, and that's to make sure I have the right address. I'm going to text you now, so just reply to that and I'll get it sent.

    If you don't see my text, check your Unknown Senders or Spam folder. On an iPhone, look for the filter or menu icon in the top right corner of your Messages app, its usually three little lines. On an Android, look for the three-dot menu. You should be able to find any filtered messages there which is where my text to you might be.

    Okay, that's all for now! Talk soon.

  2. 2

    Text them

    As soon as you hang up, send this:

    Hey [Name], it's [Your Name]! 👋 Just left you a quick voicemail. You requested a free 3 day sample of IGNYT and I want to get it in the mail to you today.

    Can you confirm this is where I'm sending it? [Address]

    Reply YES and it goes out. It's 3 single serve sticks, one a day in 8oz of water, and it comes out cran-apple.

  3. 3

    Email them too

    Send this the same day. Plenty of people read email before they answer an unknown number:

    Subject: Confirming where to send your IGNYT sample

    Hi [Name],

    You requested a free 3 day sample of IGNYT and I'm getting it packed up for you. Before it goes out, can you confirm this address is right? [Address]

    Just reply to this email and I'll drop it in the mail. Samples are limited, so the sooner you confirm the sooner yours ships.

    [Your Name]

  4. 4

    Follow up in 24 hrs

    If they don't confirm within 24 hours, text again:

    [Name]! Just following up on my text above! 👆 I still have your IGNYT sample set aside. Send me a quick yes on the address and I'll get it out to you today.

Once it is in the mail, either way 3
  1. 1

    Tell them it shipped

    Send this the day it actually goes in the mail. Not before:

    Hey [Name]! I shipped yours today, look out for it. 📦

    Can't wait to hear how you like it. One stick in 8oz of water, once a day, for three days. Use a shaker bottle or a coffee frother, it doesn't mix with a spoon. Better over ice.

  2. 2

    Email the same notice

    Send this alongside the text, so it is in both places when they go looking:

    Subject: Your IGNYT sample is on its way

    Hi [Name],

    Your free 3 day IGNYT sample went in the mail today and is on its way to you.

    When it lands, tear open one stick into 8oz of water, once a day, for three days. Shake it in a shaker bottle or use a small coffee frother, it will not dissolve with a spoon. Most people take it cold over ice. It comes out cran-apple, not a chalky shake.

    I'll check in once you have had a chance to try it. Anything you want to know before then, just reply to this.

    [Your Name]

  3. 3

    Check in a few days later

    Give it time to arrive and be tried. Five to seven days after you shipped it:

    Hey [Name]! Did your IGNYT sample land ok? 👋

    What did you think? I want to hear the honest answer, good or bad.

Tip: If they messaged you or commented, send the link rather than asking for the address in the thread. The link is what files them in your pipeline and starts their emails. An address typed into a DM exists nowhere. Personalize [Name] and [Your Name] every time, and remember some people will already have texted you the word SAMPLE from the confirmation page.
