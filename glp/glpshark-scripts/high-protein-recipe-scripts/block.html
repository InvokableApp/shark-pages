How to follow up with your High Protein Recipe Guide leads

Use these scripts immediately after receiving a new recipe guide lead.

High Protein Recipe Guide Leads
Watch first 1

Watch this before you start calling.

Do this, in order 3
  1. 1

    Call them

    They probably won't pick up, so leave this voicemail:

    Hey [Name], it's [Your Name]!

    I saw you requested our free high protein recipe guide. I'm going to text you the link now so you have it handy and can check out all the recipes.

    Also, since you're already looking at ways to add more protein and support your wellness goals, I can send you some info on an all-natural, needle-free product designed to support your body's natural GLP pathways. If you're curious, just reply back to my text. No pressure at all.

    If you don't see my text, check your Unknown Senders or Spam folder. On an iPhone, look for the filter or menu icon in the top right corner of your Messages app, its usually three little lines. On an Android, look for the three-dot menu. You should be able to find any filtered messages there which is where my text to you might be.

    Okay, that's all for now! Let me know if you received the text. Talk soon!

  2. 2

    Text them

    As soon as you hang up, send this:

    Hey [Name], it's [Your Name]! 👋 Just left you a quick voicemail. Here's the high protein recipe guide you requested so you have it handy - https://assets.cdn.filesafe.space/hKYM2WhsSBKHjvCPGkZo/media/6d26aa1b-773f-4b35-a7e7-86d6616d9027.pdf

    What if there was a natural, needle-free way to support your body's own GLP pathways that you could incorporate alongside better nutrition and a high-protein lifestyle? That's something I've been sharing with people recently, and it's definitely worth knowing about if you're working on your wellness goals.

    Curious? Reply INFO and I'll show you what it is. 👀

  3. 3

    Follow up in 24 hrs

    If they don't respond within 24 hours, text again:

    [Name]! Just following up on the above! 👆 Were you able to get the high protein recipe guide? Also, did you want me to send you the info on the all-natural GLP support product I mentioned? Shoot me a text back - I can hook you up!

Tip: Personalize [Name] and [Your Name] every time. The recipe guide link is already in the text script - just hit Copy script and send it as-is.
