How to follow up with your GLP-1 Workout Guide leads

Use these scripts immediately after receiving a new workout guide lead.

GLP-1 Workout Guide Leads
Watch first 1

Watch this before you start calling.

Do this, in order 3
  1. 1

    Call them

    They probably won't pick up, so leave this voicemail:

    Hey [Name], it's [Your Name]!

    I saw you requested our free workout guide designed to help support you while taking a GLP-1. I'm going to text you the link now so you have it handy and can start using the workouts.

    Also, since you're already being proactive about staying active while on a GLP-1, I can send you some info on an all-natural, needle-free product designed to support your body's natural GLP pathways. If you're curious, just reply back to my text. No pressure at all.

    If you don't see my text, check your Unknown Senders or Spam folder. On an iPhone, look for the filter or menu icon in the top right corner of your Messages app, its usually three little lines. On an Android, look for the three-dot menu. You should be able to find any filtered messages there which is where my text to you might be.

    Okay, that's all for now! Let me know if you received the text. Talk soon!

  2. 2

    Text them

    As soon as you hang up, send this:

    Hey [Name], it's [Your Name]! 👋 Just left you a quick voicemail.

    Here's the GLP-1 workout guide you requested so you have it handy - https://assets.cdn.filesafe.space/hKYM2WhsSBKHjvCPGkZo/media/6a8ddba4e25296bd1b285e5c.pdf

    Also, I wanted to make sure you knew about something else that may be worth looking into...

    There's a natural, needle-free solution designed to support your body's natural GLP pathways that I've been sharing with people who are focused on their fitness and wellness goals.

    You may be surprised by what it is. 👀

    Reply INFO and I'll send you a quick overview so you can see if it's something you'd be interested in.

  3. 3

    Follow up in 24 hrs

    If they don't respond within 24 hours, text again:

    [Name]! Just following up on the above! 👆 Were you able to get the workout guide? Also, did you want me to send you the info on the all-natural GLP support product I mentioned? If you're curious, shoot me a text back and I'll send it over!

Tip: Personalize [Name] and [Your Name] every time. The workout guide link is already in the text script - just hit Copy script and send it as-is.
