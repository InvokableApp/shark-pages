Workouts for home or the gym

How this guide works

Three short workouts a week, about 25 minutes each. One for your arms, one for your core and lower belly, one for your lower back and posture.

Every move has two versions:

  • At home: your bodyweight, a resistance band or a pair of dumbbells.
  • At the gym: a machine, cable or heavier weight that does the same job.

Pick whichever fits your day. You can do Monday at home and Friday at the gym and it still counts.

A straight answer about "problem areas": no exercise burns fat from one spot. Fat comes off all over, from moving more and eating well. What these workouts do is build and firm the muscle underneath those spots, so as the weight comes off, your arms, core and back look and feel stronger.

Before you start

Warm up for 5 minutes. March or walk in place, roll your shoulders, swing your arms, and do 10 slow bodyweight squats. Warm muscles move better and complain less.

Pick a weight you could lift 2 or 3 more times. The last few reps of each set should feel hard, never sloppy. If your form slips, the set is over.

Rest 45 to 60 seconds between sets.

Breathe out on the hard part of every move, and never hold your breath.

Stop if anything hurts sharply, especially your lower back. A burn in the muscle is normal; a pinch in a joint is not.

The workouts

Three workouts, one for each area

1

Workout 1: Arms

Toning the backs of your arms, your shoulders and your chest.

3 rounds of the 4 moves · about 25 minutes
  1. Tricep dip 3 x 10 to 12
  2. Bicep curl 3 x 12
  3. Push-up 3 x 8 to 12
  4. Overhead press 3 x 10 to 12
 2

Workout 2: Core and lower belly

Building the deep muscles that hold your middle in.

3 rounds of the 4 moves · about 20 minutes
  1. Dead bug 3 x 8 each side
  2. Reverse crunch 3 x 10 to 12
  3. Plank 3 x 20 to 40 seconds
  4. Bicycle crunch 3 x 10 each side
 3

Workout 3: Lower back and posture

Strengthening your lower back and glutes so you stand taller and your back feels better.

3 rounds of the 4 moves · about 25 minutes
  1. Glute bridge 3 x 12 to 15
  2. Bird dog 3 x 8 each side
  3. Superman hold 3 x 15 to 20 seconds
  4. Romanian deadlift 3 x 10
How to do them

Every move, at home or at the gym

Workout 1Arms

1.

Tricep dip

3 x 10 to 12

At homehands on the edge of a sturdy chair behind you, knees bent, lower your body by bending your elbows, then press back up.

At the gymtricep pushdown on the cable machine.

Form tipkeep your shoulders down and your elbows pointing straight back, not out to the sides.

2.

Bicep curl

3 x 12

At homestand on the middle of a resistance band, or hold two dumbbells, and curl your hands up to your shoulders.

At the gymdumbbell or cable curl.

Form tipelbows stay pinned to your sides. If you have to swing, go lighter.

3.

Push-up

3 x 8 to 12

At homehands on a kitchen counter or the edge of a couch to start, on the floor when that gets easy.

At the gymchest press machine.

Form tipone straight line from head to heels, and lower your chest, not your hips.

4.

Overhead press

3 x 10 to 12

At homedumbbells or a band under your feet, press from your shoulders to straight overhead.

At the gymseated shoulder press machine.

Form tipsqueeze your belly and glutes so your lower back doesn't arch.

Workout 2Core and lower belly

5.

Dead bug

3 x 8 each side

At homelie on your back, arms up, knees bent over your hips. Lower the opposite arm and leg toward the floor, then switch.

At the gymthe same move on a mat, holding a light dumbbell in both hands.

Form tippress your lower back into the floor the whole time. Only go as low as you can without it lifting.

6.

Reverse crunch

3 x 10 to 12

At homelie on your back, knees bent, and curl your hips up off the floor toward your ribs, then lower slowly.

At the gymhanging knee raise, or knee raise on the captain's chair.

Form tipmove slowly. The lowering is where the lower belly works.

7.

Plank

3 x 20 to 40 seconds

At homeforearms on the floor, body in a straight line, from your knees to start.

At the gymthe same, or a plank with your forearms on a stability ball for a harder version.

Form tipsqueeze your glutes and pull your belly button toward your spine. Stop when your hips sag.

8.

Bicycle crunch

3 x 10 each side

At homeon your back, hands lightly behind your head, bring one elbow toward the opposite knee as the other leg straightens.

At the gymcable woodchop, high to low.

Form tipturn from your ribs, not your neck, and keep it slow.

Workout 3Lower back and posture

9.

Glute bridge

3 x 12 to 15

At homeon your back, knees bent, feet flat, push through your heels and lift your hips until your body is straight from knees to shoulders.

At the gymhip thrust with your upper back on a bench and a dumbbell or barbell across your hips.

Form tipsqueeze your glutes at the top for a second. Your back shouldn't arch.

10.

Bird dog

3 x 8 each side

At homeon hands and knees, reach one arm forward and the opposite leg back, hold for 2 seconds, then switch.

At the gymthe same move on a mat, or a back extension on the 45 degree bench.

Form tipkeep your hips level, as if there's a glass of water on your lower back.

11.

Superman hold

3 x 15 to 20 seconds

At homelie face down and lift your arms, chest and legs a few inches off the floor. Hold, then lower.

At the gymback extension machine.

Form tipsmall and controlled. Look at the floor so your neck stays long.

12.

Romanian deadlift

3 x 10

At homedumbbells or a band under your feet. Soft knees, push your hips back and lower the weight down your legs, then stand tall.

At the gymdumbbell or barbell Romanian deadlift.

Form tipyour back stays flat and the weight stays close to your legs. You'll feel it in your hamstrings, not your lower back.

Your week

MondayWorkout 1: Arms
TuesdayA 20 to 30 minute walk
WednesdayWorkout 2: Core and lower belly
ThursdayA 20 to 30 minute walk
FridayWorkout 3: Lower back and posture
WeekendSomething you enjoy: a long walk, a bike ride, a class, the pool
Walking is the quiet hero here. It burns energy all over, it's easy on your joints, and it's the part of the week that does the most for overall fat loss.

Making it harder as you get stronger

When the last set of a move feels easy two workouts in a row, make it a little harder. Change one thing at a time:

  1. Add 2 reps to each set.
  2. Add a 4th round.
  3. Go up one band or one dumbbell size.
  4. Slow it down: 3 seconds on the way down.

Write down what you did each workout, even just in your phone's notes. Seeing the numbers go up is the best reason to keep going.

Keep going

Three workouts and a few walks a week, done consistently, will do more than any one perfect week.

Pair these workouts with the food side, eating protein at every meal and plenty of fiber, and you've got both halves of the plan working for you.
