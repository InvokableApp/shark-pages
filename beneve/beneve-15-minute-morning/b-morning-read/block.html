Fifteen minutes, three blocks of five

Fifteen minutes, in three blocks of five: fuel yourself, get focused, fill your cup.

You don't need to get up at 5. Find your 15 where it fits: before the house wakes up, while the kids eat, or right after everyone's out the door.

Every habit goes on something you already do every morning without thinking: turning off your alarm, pouring your coffee, opening the curtains. Each one comes with an anchor sentence. Say it once, out loud if you can, and the next time you do the old thing, the new one follows it.

Add one habit every two or three days and keep the ones from before. Miss a morning and pick it back up the next. A habit that keeps slipping is hung on the wrong moment, so move it.

Your first anchor sentence

After I turn off my alarm, I'll drink a full glass of water before I do anything else.

Week one · 5 minutes

Fuel yourself

Habit 1

Water before anything else

After I turn off my alarm, I'll drink a full glass of water before I do anything else.

How
Fill a glass the night before and leave it by the sink or your bed. Drink all of it before your coffee.
Why it helps
You've gone seven or eight hours without a drink. Water first is the easiest win of the day.
Habit 2

Breakfast with protein in it

After I make everyone else's breakfast, I'll make mine and sit down to eat it.

How
Eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, leftover chicken in a wrap, or a protein shake. Make one extra portion of whatever you're already making.
Why it helps
A breakfast with protein in it keeps you full longer than toast or a granola bar on the way out the door.
Habit 3

Your morning drink, sitting down

After I pour my coffee, I'll sit down for the first few sips.

How
Find one chair you go back to every morning. Drink the first few sips there, before you start clearing the counter.
Why it helps
Those three minutes are yours, before the day starts asking for things.
Week two · 5 minutes

Get focused

Habit 4

Your top 3 on paper

After I sit down with my coffee, I'll write my top 3 for today.

How
A notebook that lives next to your chair. Write three things. Not 20 things, just three.
Why it helps
Three is a list you can finish by dinner.
Habit 5

One of the three is for you

After I write my top 3, I'll check that one of them is for me.

How
A walk at lunch, a call with a friend, a real lunch, bed by ten. If none of the three is yours, swap the smallest one out.
Why it helps
If it isn't on the list, it doesn't happen. This puts your health on the list every day.
Habit 6

Phone face down until the list is done

After I pick up my phone from the charger, I'll put it face down until my top 3 is written.

How
Turn it over, or leave it on the charger in the kitchen. Messages will still be there in five minutes.
Why it helps
Read your own list first, then everyone else's.
Week three · 5 minutes

Fill your cup

Habit 7

Five minutes that are just for you

After I finish my top 3, I'll take five minutes for me.

How
Pray, stretch, put on a favorite song, read a page, or sit in the quiet. Pick one and do the same one all week.
Why it helps
This is the part of the 15 that fills your cup.
Habit 8

A minute of morning light

After I open the curtains, I'll step outside or stand at the door for one minute.

How
Look at the sky, phone in your pocket. On a cold or rainy day, stand at the window.
Why it helps
Morning light helps set your body clock, which helps you feel awake now and sleepy tonight.
Habit 9

One stretch

After I brush my teeth, I'll reach up tall, fold forward and roll my shoulders.

How
Reach for the ceiling for three slow breaths, fold forward with soft knees for three more, then roll your shoulders back five times.
Why it helps
A minute is enough to undo a night of sleeping curled up.
Week four · 15 minutes

Make it yours

Habit 10

The whole 15, in your order

After I turn off my alarm, I'll start my 15.

How
Put the three blocks in the order that fits your house. Before the kids wake up: fuel, focus, fill. After drop-off: fill, fuel, focus.
Why it helps
Any order works, as long as it's most mornings.
Habit 11

Read back the week

After I pour my Sunday coffee, I'll read back the week's check-ins.

How
Look for your best-energy days. What did you do that morning? Circle anything that shows up more than once.
Why it helps
Your own week shows you what works for you.
Habit 12

Keep what worked

On day 30, I'll pick the habits I'm keeping.

How
Keep the ones that fit your mornings. Drop the ones that never did. Copy the keepers onto the fridge page.
Why it helps
The keepers are the ones that already fit your mornings.

For the hard mornings

The 5-minute version

For the mornings that fall apart: the sick kid, the early meeting, the alarm that didn't go off.

  • A glass of water
  • Your top 3, written on anything
  • Three slow breaths before you walk out the door
