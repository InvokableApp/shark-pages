Influencer Aptitude Test

Brands will pay you to represent them. Find the brands that match your expertise.

Role specific 2 min 10 questions No experience needed

Summary of the assessment

It works like a job application run backwards. You are not selling yourself to anyone here. Ten questions work out whether being an influencer already fits the way you live, and which version of it fits you best.

None of it is about sales experience. It scores the four things listed here, and at the end you get your profile and the path that matches it. Two minutes, and there are no wrong answers.

What it measures

  • Influence and trust. Whether people in your life already come to you before they make a decision.
  • Your health story. What you have lived through that someone else is living right now.
  • Conversation comfort. How easily you talk to people about something that worked for you.
  • Market timing. How close you already are to the conversations people are having about hormones, gut health and energy.

Free, and you do not need an account. Your result shows as soon as you finish.
