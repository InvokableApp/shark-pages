The ninety days that cost nothing

The first habit on this list outperforms every product on this page. It is also the one almost nobody does, which is why it is first.

  1. Every morning

    Twenty five to thirty grams of protein at breakfast

    Not at dinner. Structural tissue gets built from what is available across the day, and most people front-load carbohydrate and back-load protein. This one change is the highest-leverage thing on this page, and it is free.

  2. Every day

    Vitamin C in the same meal

    Collagen synthesis is vitamin C dependent. A kiwi, half a pepper or a handful of berries alongside the protein costs nothing and is the difference between raw material arriving and raw material being usable.

  3. Twice a week

    Lift something heavy

    Resistance training is the strongest non-dietary signal your body has for building and holding connective tissue. Twice a week, forty minutes, is enough to change the trajectory.

  4. Every day

    Water with electrolytes, not just water

    Skin that has stopped bouncing back is often skin that is chronically under-hydrated at the cellular level. Plain water passes through. A pinch of salt in the first glass of the day changes how much of it stays.

  5. Most days

    Eat the collagen, do not only drink it

    Bone broth, slow-cooked cuts on the bone, skin-on fish, chicken with the skin. This is where collagen came from before it came in a bottle, and it arrives with the cofactors attached.

  6. Weeks 1 to 4

    Protect the sleep window

    Growth hormone release is concentrated in the first part of the night, and that is when repair happens. A consistent bedtime does more for elasticity than an extra hour on an irregular one.

  7. Day 1, then day 90

    Take the photo today

    Same window, same time of day, no makeup, no filter. You cannot see a twelve-week change in a mirror you look into every morning, and without the photo you will conclude nothing worked when something did.

What not to spend money on

Being told what to skip is worth more than being told what to buy, and it is the part almost nobody puts on a page like this one.

  • High-dose biotinIt is the default hair supplement and it has a real problem: high-dose biotin interferes with several common lab assays, including thyroid and cardiac tests. If your hair changed, that is a reason to get accurate labs, not to blur them.
  • Collagen drinks that are mostly sugarRead the panel before the front of the bottle. If the collagen figure is buried under a sweetener list, you are buying a soft drink with a claim on it.
  • Judging your hair before month threeA follicle cycle is considerably longer than a skin cycle. Anything you start this week shows up in your hair in the autumn, not next month.
