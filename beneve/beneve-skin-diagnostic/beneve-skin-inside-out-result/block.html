Ten free habits, starting with breakfast

The first one outperforms every product on this page. It's also the one almost nobody does, which is why it's first.

  1. Every morning

    Twenty five to thirty grams of protein at breakfast

    Not at dinner. Your body builds structural tissue from what's available across the day, and most of us front load the carbohydrate and leave the protein until the evening. It's the one change on this page that does the most, and it costs nothing.

  2. Every day

    Vitamin C in the same meal

    Collagen synthesis depends on it. A kiwi, half a pepper or a handful of berries next to the protein costs nothing, and it's the difference between the raw material arriving and the raw material being usable.

  3. Twice a week

    Lift something heavy

    Resistance training is the strongest signal your body has for building and holding connective tissue. Twice a week, forty minutes, is enough to change the direction of travel.

  4. Most days

    Eat collagen, don't only drink it

    Bone broth, slow cooked cuts on the bone, skin on fish, chicken with the skin left on. This is where collagen came from before it came in a bottle, and it arrives with its cofactors attached.

  5. Weeks 1 to 3

    Take the ultra processed food out for three weeks

    Seed oils, refined sugar, anything with a label longer than the food. Three weeks is long enough to notice and short enough that you'll actually finish it. Keep whatever helped, and put back whatever you missed.

  6. Three weeks, if you break out

    If you get spots, try dropping dairy first

    It's the one women report most often, by a distance. Give it three weeks, then put it back and pay attention, because the week you bring it back tells you more than the three weeks without it did.

  7. Every day

    Put a pinch of salt in your first glass of water

    Skin that's stopped bouncing back is often skin that's quietly under hydrated at the cell level, and plain water passes straight through. A pinch of salt in the first glass changes how much of it stays.

  8. Weeks 1 to 4

    Protect the first half of the night

    Growth hormone release is concentrated early in the night, and that's when repair happens. A consistent bedtime does more for elasticity than an extra hour on an irregular one.

  9. Ten minutes daily

    Meditate with a relaxed face

    Jaw unclenched, brow soft, tongue off the roof of your mouth. Most of us hold tension all day in exactly the places we're trying to smooth. Ten minutes is free, you can do it anywhere, and it's the only thing on this page that gets easier the longer you do it.

  10. Today, then day 90

    Take the photo today

    Same window, same time of day, no makeup, no filter. You can't see a twelve week change in a mirror you look into every morning, and without the photo you'll decide nothing happened when something did.

What not to spend money on

Here's what we'd leave on the shelf, and why.

  • High-dose biotinIt's the default hair supplement and it has a real problem: high-dose biotin interferes with several common lab assays, including thyroid and cardiac tests. If your hair changed, that's a reason to get accurate labs.
  • Collagen drinks that are mostly sugarRead the panel before you read the front of the bottle. If the collagen figure is buried under a sweetener list, you're buying a soft drink with a claim on it.
  • Judging your hair before month threeA follicle cycle is much longer than a skin cycle. What you start this week shows up in your hair in the autumn.
