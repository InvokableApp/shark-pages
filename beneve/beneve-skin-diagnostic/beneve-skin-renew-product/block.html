The quad peptide complex

Four peptide technologies, one formula

01

GHK-Cu and Matrixyl 3000

Beneve groups these two under long-term support: skin resilience and healthy-looking skin.

GHK-Cu is a naturally occurring copper-binding peptide, described by the brand as supporting skin conditioning, barrier support, hydration retention and skin renewal. This is the slow half of the formula, which is why the directions say consistently.

02

Argireline Amplified and DMAE

The brand's mid-term group: texture and appearance support, alongside Matrixyl and hyaluronic acid.

Argireline is a trademark of The Lubrizol Corporation or its affiliates. Beneve's own three-tier framing is worth reading before you judge week one: short term is hydration, mid term is texture, long term is resilience.

03

DMAE and hyaluronic acid

The short-term pair. Beneve puts hydration and smoother-looking skin in this group.

This is the part you are most likely to notice first, and the reason the serum feels like it is doing something before the peptides have had time to.

04

Designed to pair, not to work alone

Renew was intentionally formulated to pair synergistically with Luxe Liquid Collagen.

That is Beneve's own sentence, not ours, and it is the honest caveat on a topical: a serum works on the surface. If your diagnostic pointed at hydration and elasticity from the inside as well, Luxe is the other half.
