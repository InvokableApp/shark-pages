Ten free habits, starting with breakfast

The first one does more than any product on this page. It's also the one almost nobody does, which is why it's first.

Start here, and start free
Every morning

Twenty five to thirty grams of protein at breakfast

Your body builds skin, hair and nails out of the protein it has to hand that day, and most of us put the carbohydrate at breakfast and leave the protein until dinner. This is the change that does the most, and it costs nothing extra.

  • What thirty grams looks like. Three eggs with a bit of cheese. A tin of tuna. Greek yogurt with seeds. A scoop of whey in milk on the rushed mornings.
  • What doesn't get close. A bowl of cereal is about six grams, and toast with jam is under five.
  • Aim for the same again at lunch and dinner. Breakfast is the one worth fixing first, because it's the one that's usually empty.
  • Give it a month before you judge it. Nails are usually the first thing women notice changing.
Every day

Vitamin C in the same meal as the protein

Your body can't make collagen without it, so the raw material and the vitamin need to turn up together. This is the cheapest multiplier in the plan.

  • What counts. A kiwi, half a red pepper, a handful of berries, an orange, a squeeze of lemon over the greens.
  • Timing. Same meal as the protein, not later in the day.
  • Food before a capsule. A capsule works. Fruit brings everything else the plant had in it along with the vitamin.
Twice a week

Lift something heavy twice a week

Resistance training is the strongest signal your body has for building and holding the tissue underneath your skin, and it works on the same raw materials as the protein habit.

  • Twice a week, forty minutes. Two sessions you keep beat five you dread.
  • Heavy for you. The last two reps of each set should be hard. If they aren't, the weight is too light.
  • Where to start. Squat, hinge, push, pull, carry. A gym induction or any beginner's program covers all five.
  • Walking is good for you and it isn't this. Keep the walks, add the weights.
Most days

Eat collagen, don't only drink it

This is where collagen came from before it came in a bottle, and it arrives with everything else that was in the animal.

  • What to eat. Bone broth, slow cooked cuts on the bone, oxtail, chicken with the skin left on, fish with the skin on.
  • The easiest version. A slow cooker on Sunday covers three dinners and makes the broth at the same time.
  • How to spot real broth. It sets like jelly in the fridge. Stock from a carton that stays liquid is mostly salt.
Weeks 1 to 3

Take ultra processed food out for three weeks

Ultra processed means food built in a factory, not cooked in a kitchen. You're changing what you buy, not buying more, and three weeks is short enough to finish and long enough to see something.

  • Swap the oils. Butter, olive oil or coconut oil in place of sunflower, canola, soybean and anything sold as vegetable oil.
  • Read one line on the packet. If the ingredients list is longer than the food, put it back on the shelf.
  • Keep the list short. Meat, fish, eggs, vegetables, fruit, rice, potatoes, butter, olive oil. All fine, and they always were.
  • Then add things back one at a time. The week you put something back teaches you more than the three weeks without it.
  • What women mention first. Less puffiness around the eyes in the morning, usually somewhere in the second week.
Three weeks, if you break out

If you get spots, try dropping dairy first

Of everything women try for breakouts, dairy is the one they report back on most often, by a distance. Give it three weeks, then put it back and pay attention.

  • What counts. Milk, cheese, yogurt, cream, and the splash in your coffee, which is the one everybody forgets.
  • What to drink instead. Unsweetened oat, almond or coconut milk. Check the label, because a lot of the flavoured ones are mostly sugar.
  • Butter is usually fine. It's nearly all fat, with very little of the milk protein left in it.
  • The test is the week you bring it back. One glass of milk or a bowl of yogurt, then watch the next two days.
Every day

A pinch of salt in your first glass of water

Skin that's stopped bouncing back is often skin that's quietly short of water, and plain water on an empty stomach goes straight through you.

  • How much. A small pinch of sea salt in the first glass of the day. It should barely taste of anything.
  • Then keep sipping. Steady through the day does what two big glasses in the morning can't.
  • Skip this one if you've been told to watch your salt. Ask your doctor first, and do the rest of the list regardless.
Weeks 1 to 4

Protect the first half of the night

Repair is concentrated in the first few hours of sleep, so the hours before midnight are the ones that show up in your skin.

  • Same bedtime seven nights a week. Weekends included. Consistency does more here than an extra hour on an irregular schedule.
  • Phone out of the room, or at least off the bed. Buy a cheap alarm clock and stop using your phone as one.
  • Cool and dark. Blackout curtains are worth more for sleep quality than most supplements.
  • Nothing to drink within three hours of bed. Alcohol gets you to sleep quickly and then takes the deep part of the night away.
Ten minutes daily

Ten minutes a day with your face relaxed

Most of us hold tension all day in the exact places we're trying to smooth: the jaw, the brow, the space between the eyes. Ten minutes of sitting still with all of it loose is free, and it's the only thing on this page that gets easier the longer you do it.

  • How to sit. Jaw unclenched, teeth apart, brow soft, tongue off the roof of your mouth, shoulders down.
  • What to do. Breathe in through your nose for four, out for six. When your mind wanders, come back to your jaw.
  • When. The same ten minutes every day beats a longer sit you have to find time for. First coffee, lunch break, or the car before you go in.
  • Then check in during the day. Set one alarm for the afternoon. Whatever your face is doing when it goes off is what it's been doing for hours.
Today, then day 90

Take the photo today

You can't see a twelve week change in a mirror you look into every morning. Without a photo from day one, you'll decide nothing happened when something did.

  • Same window, same time of day. Daylight from the side, not the overhead bathroom light, which flattens everything.
  • No makeup, no filter, hair back. One straight on, one from each side.
  • Put a reminder in your phone for day 90. Then leave the first photo alone until it goes off.

What not to spend money on

Here's what we'd leave on the shelf, and why.

  • High-dose biotinIt's the default hair supplement and it has a real problem: at high doses it interferes with several common lab tests, including thyroid and cardiac ones. If your hair changed, you want accurate results, not blurry ones.
  • Collagen drinks that are mostly sugarRead the panel on the back before you read the front of the bottle. If the collagen figure is buried under a sweetener list, you're buying a soft drink with a claim on it.
  • Judging your hair before month threeA hair follicle works on a much longer cycle than skin. What you start this week shows up in your hair in the autumn.
