Beyond collagen alone

Five things in every tablespoon

01

Two collagen types, 500mg

Wild caught marine collagen peptides (Type I) and hydrolyzed chicken collagen peptides (Type II).

Beneve's framing: Type I is the most abundant type in the body and is the one they associate with hydration, elasticity, hair and nails. Type II is the one they associate with cartilage and skin structure. Both are hydrolyzed, which is what the brand means by easily absorbed.

02

Hyaluronic acid, 120mg

Collagen provides structure, hyaluronic acid helps maintain moisture. Together they support supple-looking skin.

Worth noticing that hyaluronic acid appears in both products. The serum puts it on the surface, this puts it in the daily dose, and that overlap is the point of the pairing rather than a duplication to avoid.

03

Yuth spermidine, 15mg

A compound studied for its role in supporting cellular processes and overall wellbeing.

Yuth is patent-pending and under exclusive global distribution by Compound Solutions, Inc. It is the ingredient that separates this from a plain collagen, and it is the one the brand leads with on the label.

04

Chondroitin sulfate and MCT oil, 25mg each

Chondroitin works alongside collagen for joints and mobility. MCT oil helps the body absorb and use the rest of the blend.

Sweetened with steviol glycosides and monk fruit, flavoured orange vanilla. Gluten free, dairy free, nut free and GMO free, and it contains fish (cod).
