Twelve free habits, one a week

All three came back high, so this is the long version. Add them roughly one a week and let the free ones do their work before you buy anything. Starting all twelve on Monday is the most common way this plan gets dropped by Friday.

Start here, and start free
Week 1

Twenty five to thirty grams of protein at breakfast

Your body builds skin, hair and nails out of the protein it has on hand that day, and most of us put the carbohydrate at breakfast and leave the protein until dinner. This is the change that does the most, and it costs nothing extra.

  • What thirty grams looks like. Three eggs with a bit of cheese. A can of tuna. Greek yogurt with seeds. A scoop of whey in milk on the rushed mornings.
  • What doesn't get close. A bowl of cereal is about six grams, and toast with jam is under five.
  • Aim for the same again at lunch and dinner. Breakfast is the one worth fixing first, because it's the one that's usually empty.
  • Give it a month before you judge it. Nails are usually the first thing to change.
Week 1

Mineral sunscreen, every single morning

Nothing else on this list does as much for tone and texture. Sun is the biggest driver of the change you described, and window glass doesn't stop all of it, so grey days and desk days count.

  • Check the back of the bottle. Zinc oxide or titanium dioxide, SPF 30 or higher. Those are the mineral filters.
  • How much. About half a teaspoon for your face and neck, which is more than most of us use.
  • Where it goes in the routine. Last step of the morning, after moisturizer and before makeup.
  • Don't stop at your jaw. Neck, chest and the backs of your hands show it first and are the hardest to put right later.
Week 2

Vitamin C in the same meal as the protein

Your body can't make collagen without vitamin C, so eat it in the same meal as the protein.

  • What counts. A kiwi, half a red pepper, a handful of berries, an orange, a squeeze of lemon over the greens.
  • Timing. Same meal as the protein, not later in the day.
  • Food before a capsule. A capsule works. Fruit brings everything else the plant had in it along with the vitamin.
Week 3

Get the harsh ingredients off your face

So much of what gets called sensitive skin is a reaction to something already in the routine. The only way to find out is to take the suspects away for three weeks, then bring them back one at a time.

  • What you're looking for on the back of the bottle. Fragrance or parfum, sulfates (sodium lauryl or laureth sulfate), and alcohol denat high up in the list.
  • Don't throw anything out. Put it in a drawer for three weeks. If your skin doesn't change, you've lost nothing and you can stop wondering about it.
  • Start with what sits on your face longest. Your moisturizer and serum are on all day. A cleanser is on for thirty seconds, so it's the least likely culprit.
  • Add them back one at a time, a week apart. Bring all of them back on the same day and you've learned nothing.
Week 4

Take ultra processed food out for three weeks

Ultra processed means food made in a factory. You're only changing what you buy, and three weeks is short enough to finish and long enough to see something.

  • Swap the oils. Butter, olive oil or coconut oil in place of sunflower, canola, soybean and anything sold as vegetable oil.
  • Read the ingredients on the package. If the ingredients list is longer than the food, put it back on the shelf.
  • Keep the list short. Meat, fish, eggs, vegetables, fruit, rice, potatoes, butter, olive oil. All fine.
  • Then add things back one at a time. The week you put something back teaches you more than the three weeks without it.
  • What you might notice first. Less puffiness around the eyes in the morning, usually somewhere in the second week.
Week 5

Two alcohol free nights a week

Alcohol costs you sleep quality and hydration on the same night, and those are the two things that show on your face most.

  • Pick the two nights now. Put them on the calendar so you aren't deciding at six o'clock.
  • Have the replacement in the fridge. Soda, lime and bitters in a nice glass works well.
  • Watch the morning after the nights you do drink. Compare your skin with the mornings after your nights off.
Week 6

Ten minutes a day with your face relaxed

Most of us hold tension all day in the exact places we're trying to smooth: the jaw, the brow, the space between the eyes. Ten minutes of sitting still with all of it loose is free, and it gets easier the longer you do it.

  • How to sit. Jaw unclenched, teeth apart, brow soft, tongue off the roof of your mouth, shoulders down.
  • What to do. Breathe in through your nose for four, out for six. When your mind wanders, come back to your jaw.
  • When. The same ten minutes every day beats a longer sit you have to find time for. First coffee, lunch break, or the car before you go in.
  • Then check in during the day. Set one alarm for the afternoon. Whatever your face is doing when it goes off is what it's been doing for hours.
Week 7

Lift something heavy twice a week

Lifting weights is the strongest signal your body gets to build and hold the tissue underneath your skin, and it puts the protein from breakfast to use.

  • Twice a week, forty minutes. Two sessions you keep beat five you dread.
  • Heavy for you. The last two reps of each set should be hard. If they aren't, the weight is too light.
  • Where to start. Squat, hinge, push, pull, carry. A trainer's intro session or any beginner's program covers all five.
  • Walking doesn't count here. Keep the walks and add the weights.
Week 8

Broccoli, cabbage or cauliflower most days

DIM comes from this group of vegetables, and it's the one most of us skip.

  • The list. Broccoli, cabbage, Brussels sprouts, kale, cauliflower, arugula.
  • How much. About a cupful, most days.
  • Roasted in olive oil with salt counts. So does frozen. A bag of frozen broccoli and five minutes in a pan is the whole job.
Week 9

Then get them out of the rest of the house

Your face spends eight hours a night on your pillowcase and all day in the air you scented. That's more contact time than anything you put on your face. Skip this step and step one can look like it didn't work.

  • Laundry first. Unscented detergent, no fabric softener, no dryer sheets. Softener is designed to leave a coating behind, and that coating spends the night on your cheek.
  • Then the air. Candles, plug-ins, room sprays and diffusers all end up on your skin too.
  • Cleaning products. Swap the scented bathroom spray for something unscented, and wear gloves for anything with bleach in it.
  • While you're testing, wash the pillowcase twice a week. It's the single piece of fabric with the most contact time.
Three weeks, if you break out

If you break out, try dropping dairy first

For breakouts, dairy is the first thing to try cutting. Give it three weeks, then put it back and pay attention.

  • What counts. Milk, cheese, yogurt, cream, and the splash in your coffee, which is the one everybody forgets.
  • What to drink instead. Unsweetened oat, almond or coconut milk. Check the label, because a lot of the flavored ones are mostly sugar.
  • Butter is usually fine. It's nearly all fat, with very little of the milk protein left in it.
  • The test is the week you bring it back. One glass of milk or a bowl of yogurt, then watch the next two days.
Today, then day 90

Take the photo today

You can't see a twelve week change in a mirror you look into every morning. Without a photo from day one, you'll decide nothing happened when something did.

  • Same window, same time of day. Daylight from the side, not the overhead bathroom light, which flattens everything.
  • No makeup, no filter, hair back. One straight on, one from each side.
  • Put a reminder in your phone for day 90. Then leave the first photo alone until it goes off.

What not to spend money on

Here's what we'd leave on the shelf, and why.

  • Starting all three products in the same weekYou won't know which one did anything, and if something irritates you, you won't know what to stop. Stagger them the way the habits are staggered.
  • Anything promising results in a weekSkin takes about a month to turn over, and hair works on a much longer cycle. Anything you see in seven days is water.
  • High-dose biotinAt high doses it interferes with several common lab tests, including thyroid ones. If your hair changed, you want accurate results.
  • Juice cleanses and detox teasYour liver and kidneys already do that job. The teas that make a visible difference do it with a laxative.
