Start here, and start free

The ninety days that cost nothing

Seven habits, in the order that matters. Every one of them is free, and together they do more for surface texture in twelve weeks than any product does on its own.

  1. Weeks 1 to 2

    Stop stripping it

    Swap the foaming cleanser for a non-foaming one and stop washing your face in hot water. A great deal of what reads as fine lines at the end of the day is a dehydrated barrier, and a barrier recovers in about two weeks once you stop taking it apart every morning.

  2. Every day

    Apply to damp skin, inside sixty seconds

    Pat, do not rub, and put the next layer on while your face is still slightly wet. Water is what plumps the skin. Everything you apply on top is the lid, and a lid works better on a full jar.

  3. Every day

    Sunscreen every morning, indoor days included

    Nothing else on this list comes close. Ultraviolet exposure is the single largest driver of the texture change you described, and window glass does not stop all of it.

  4. Weeks 3 to 8

    Add ONE active, three nights a week

    One. Not three. Give it eight weeks before you decide anything. Stacking new actives together is how people end up irritated, unable to tell which one caused it, and convinced their skin is sensitive.

  5. Every day

    Neck and the backs of your hands

    Whatever goes on your face goes there too, in the same motion. They age faster than the face and get treated the least, and they are the two places people actually notice.

  6. Every night

    Change how you sleep on it

    Silk or satin pillowcase, and off your face. Eight hours a night of a cotton case pressing creases into one cheek shows up as asymmetry long before it shows up as ageing.

  7. Day 1, then day 90

    Take the photo today

    Same window, same time of day, no makeup, no filter. You cannot see a twelve-week change in a mirror you look into every morning, and without the photo you will conclude nothing worked when something did.

What not to spend money on

Being told what to skip is worth more than being told what to buy, and it is the part almost nobody puts on a page like this one.

  • Anything promising results in a weekSkin turns over in roughly a month, and the collagen story is measured in quarters. A seven-day claim is a claim about water, not about structure.
  • A second expensive eye creamWhile the rest of your routine is a cleanser, an eye cream is the most expensive way to make almost no difference. Fix the whole face first.
  • Toning and micro-current devices, for nowThey are not nothing, but they are the last five percent. Buying one before the basics are in place is buying the roof before the walls.
