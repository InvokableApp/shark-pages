Start here, and start free

Nine free habits, starting with what you take off your face

Not one of these costs anything, and most of them are about taking something away. Together they do more for texture in twelve weeks than a product does on its own.

  1. Week 1

    Get the harsh chemicals off your face

    Fragrance, sulphates, drying alcohols, and anything whose label says parfum without saying what's in it. So much of what we call sensitive skin is a reaction to the routine, and the only way to find out is to take the suspects away for a few weeks.

  2. Every day

    Cut your routine down to three things

    Cleanser, something to hold water in, sunscreen. A short routine on calm skin beats a long routine on irritated skin, and it costs less. Anything you add after that has to earn its place by being the only thing you changed that week.

  3. Every day

    Wash in water that isn't hot

    Hot water strips the oil your barrier is made of, which is why skin feels tight after a shower and looks worse by the evening. Lukewarm, and pat dry. It's free, and most of us undo it every single morning.

  4. Every morning

    Sunscreen, every single day

    Nothing else on this list comes close for texture. Ultraviolet exposure is the biggest driver of the change you described, and window glass doesn't stop all of it, so grey days and desk days count too.

  5. Weeks 1 to 3

    Take the ultra processed food out for three weeks

    Seed oils, refined sugar, anything with a label longer than the food. Three weeks is long enough to notice and short enough that you'll actually finish it. Keep whatever helped, and put back whatever you missed.

  6. Ten minutes daily

    Meditate with a relaxed face

    Jaw unclenched, brow soft, tongue off the roof of your mouth. Most of us hold tension all day in exactly the places we're trying to smooth. Ten minutes is free, you can do it anywhere, and it's the only thing on this page that gets easier the longer you do it.

  7. Every night

    Sleep on clean fabric, off your face

    Whatever is in your detergent spends eight hours pressed against your cheek, and a cotton case creases skin that's already dehydrated. Unscented wash, and sleep on your back if you can learn to.

  8. Week 2

    Then get them out of the rest of the house

    Laundry detergent, cleaning sprays, candles, plug-ins. Your face spends eight hours a night on the sheets you washed and all day in the air you scented, which is more contact time than anything you put on it on purpose.

  9. Today, then day 90

    Take the photo today

    Same window, same time of day, no makeup, no filter. You can't see a twelve week change in a mirror you look into every morning, and without the photo you'll decide nothing happened when something did.

What not to spend money on

Here's what we'd leave on the shelf, and why.

  • Anything promising results in a weekSkin turns over in about a month, and collagen is measured in seasons. A seven day claim is a claim about water.
  • A second expensive eye creamWhile the rest of your routine is one cleanser, an eye cream is the most expensive way to change almost nothing. Look after the whole face first.
  • Micro-current and toning devices, for nowThey aren't nothing, they're the last five percent. Buying one before the basics are in place is buying the roof before the walls.
