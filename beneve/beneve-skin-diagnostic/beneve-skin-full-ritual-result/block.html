Start here, and start free

Twelve free habits, one a week

Every lane came back high, so this is the long version. Add them roughly one a week and let the free ones do their work before you buy anything. Starting all twelve on Monday is the most common way this plan gets abandoned by Friday.

  1. Week 1

    Twenty five to thirty grams of protein at breakfast

    The one habit that does the most, and it's first because everything structural after it needs the raw material to be there. Free, and it takes no extra time once it's a habit.

  2. Week 1

    Sunscreen every morning

    The other free one that outperforms almost anything you could buy. Grey days and desk days included.

  3. Week 2

    Vitamin C with the protein

    Collagen synthesis depends on it. Berries, a kiwi, half a pepper. The cheapest multiplier in the plan.

  4. Week 3

    Get the harsh chemicals off your face

    Fragrance, sulphates, drying alcohols, and anything whose label says parfum without saying what's in it. So much of what we call sensitive skin is a reaction to the routine, and the only way to find out is to take the suspects away for a few weeks.

  5. Week 4

    Take the ultra processed food out for three weeks

    Seed oils, refined sugar, anything with a label longer than the food. Three weeks is long enough to notice and short enough that you'll actually finish it. Keep whatever helped, and put back whatever you missed.

  6. Week 5

    Two alcohol free nights

    Two, not zero. It buys back the sleep quality and the hydration on the same night, and it's the load habit women actually keep.

  7. Week 6

    Meditate with a relaxed face

    Jaw unclenched, brow soft, tongue off the roof of your mouth. Most of us hold tension all day in exactly the places we're trying to smooth. Ten minutes is free, you can do it anywhere, and it's the only thing on this page that gets easier the longer you do it.

  8. Week 7

    Resistance training twice a week

    The strongest signal your body has for building and holding connective tissue, and it works the same lane as the protein.

  9. Week 8

    Cruciferous vegetables most days

    Broccoli, cabbage, Brussels sprouts, kale. The food group behind the load lane, and the one most of us skip.

  10. Week 9

    Then get them out of the rest of the house

    Laundry detergent, cleaning sprays, candles, plug-ins. Your face spends eight hours a night on the sheets you washed and all day in the air you scented, which is more contact time than anything you put on it on purpose.

  11. Three weeks, if you break out

    If you get spots, try dropping dairy first

    It's the one women report most often, by a distance. Give it three weeks, then put it back and pay attention, because the week you bring it back tells you more than the three weeks without it did.

  12. Today, then day 90

    Take the photo today

    Same window, same time of day, no makeup, no filter. You can't see a twelve week change in a mirror you look into every morning, and without the photo you'll decide nothing happened when something did.

What not to spend money on

Here's what we'd leave on the shelf, and why.

  • Starting all three products in the same weekYou won't know which one did anything, and if something irritates you won't know what to stop. Stagger them the way the habits are staggered.
  • Anything promising results in a weekSkin turns over in about a month, and hair on a much longer cycle. A seven day claim is a claim about water.
  • High-dose biotinIt interferes with several common lab assays, including thyroid tests. If your hair changed, get accurate labs.
  • Juice cleanses and detox teasThe organs that do that job are already installed. The teas that produce a visible result do it with a laxative.
