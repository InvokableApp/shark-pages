Start here, and start free

The ninety days that cost nothing

Eight habits across three lanes. Add them in this order, roughly one a week. Trying to start all eight on Monday is the most common way this plan gets abandoned by Friday.

  1. Week 1

    Twenty five to thirty grams of protein at breakfast

    The single highest-leverage habit on this page, and the first because everything structural downstream depends on the raw material being there. Free, and it takes no extra time once it is a habit.

  2. Week 1

    Sunscreen every morning

    The other free one that outperforms almost everything you could buy. Grey days and indoor days included.

  3. Week 2

    Vitamin C with the protein

    Collagen synthesis depends on it. Berries, a kiwi, half a pepper. It is the cheapest multiplier in the plan.

  4. Week 3

    Apply to damp skin, inside sixty seconds

    Whatever your routine currently is, this change alone makes it work better. Water plumps, product seals.

  5. Week 4

    Two alcohol-free nights

    Not zero, two. It buys back the sleep quality and the hydration on the same night, and it is the load habit people actually keep.

  6. Week 5

    Resistance training twice a week

    The strongest signal your body has for building and holding connective tissue, and it works on the same lane as the protein.

  7. Week 6

    Cruciferous vegetables most days

    Broccoli, cabbage, Brussels sprouts, kale. The food group behind the load lane, and the one most people skip.

  8. Day 1, then day 90

    Take the photo today

    Same window, same time of day, no makeup, no filter. You cannot see a twelve-week change in a mirror you look into every morning, and without the photo you will conclude nothing worked when something did.

What not to spend money on

Being told what to skip is worth more than being told what to buy, and it is the part almost nobody puts on a page like this one.

  • Starting all three products in the same weekYou will not know which one did anything, and if something irritates you will not know what to stop. Stagger them the way the habits are staggered.
  • Anything promising results in a weekSkin turns over in about a month, hair on a considerably longer cycle. A seven-day claim is a claim about water.
  • High-dose biotinIt interferes with several common lab assays, including thyroid tests. If your hair changed, get accurate labs rather than blurred ones.
  • Juice cleanses and detox teasThe organs that do that job are already installed. The teas that produce a visible result do it with a laxative.

Three lanes, one at a time

The reason this result exists as its own routine is that people with all three lanes lit tend to do everything at once and stop within a month. One habit a week, one product a month, and a photograph on day one. That is the whole method.
