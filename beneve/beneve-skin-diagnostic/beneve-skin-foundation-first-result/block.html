Your routine

Foundation First

A dull, tired look on rested skin is rarely a skincare problem. Your answers point at load: the things your body is spending its resources processing before any of it reaches your face.

What your answers pointed at

Three lanes. Almost everyone has one that leads, and the plan below is ordered by yours.

Systemic loadLead

Stress, sleep debt, sun, alcohol. This is where your answers concentrated, and it is the one lane that nothing you apply to your face can reach.

Surface

Moderate. Worth keeping simple until the load comes down, because irritated skin looks duller, not brighter.

Internal supply

Moderate. Supportive rather than urgent for you, and it becomes more important the longer the load stays high.

Start here, and start free

The ninety days that cost nothing

Do not try to change all four loads at once. Pick the one you would genuinely keep, then let the rest of the list carry the weight.

  1. Week 1

    Pick one load, honestly

    Sleep, alcohol, sun or stress. Choose the one you would actually keep changed in three months rather than the one you think you should choose. A habit you abandon in week two contributes nothing.

  2. Every day

    Sunscreen, even on the grey days

    Uneven tone and a dull cast are the two things sun exposure reliably produces, and they are the two you described. This is the highest-return thirty seconds in the whole plan.

  3. Most days

    Cruciferous vegetables on the plate

    Broccoli, cabbage, Brussels sprouts, kale, cauliflower. This is the food group your body draws DIM from, and it is the one most people skip entirely. Roasted with oil and salt is fine. It does not have to be virtuous to count.

  4. Every day

    Colour, not supplements

    Berries, leafy greens, peppers, tomatoes, anything deeply coloured. Antioxidant capacity from food arrives with everything else the plant had in it, which no capsule reproduces.

  5. Weeks 1 to 4

    Two alcohol-free nights a week

    Not zero. Two. Alcohol costs you sleep quality and hydration on the same night, and those are the two inputs your complexion reads most directly. Two nights is a change people keep.

  6. Every morning

    Ten minutes of daylight before screens

    It sets the clock that governs when you get tired, which governs the sleep that governs how you look. Standing outside with a coffee counts.

  7. Day 1, then day 90

    Take the photo today

    Same window, same time of day, no makeup, no filter. You cannot see a twelve-week change in a mirror you look into every morning, and without the photo you will conclude nothing worked when something did.

What not to spend money on

Being told what to skip is worth more than being told what to buy, and it is the part almost nobody puts on a page like this one.

  • Juice cleanses and detox teasYour liver and kidneys are the detox system. The teas that produce a visible result do it with a laxative, and the weight you lose is the water you needed.
  • Brightening actives on irritated skinIf the barrier is already struggling, a brightening acid makes the dullness worse before it makes it better. Get the load down and the routine simple first.
  • A dermaroller, or anything that breaks the skin at homeThe risk profile at home is nothing like the risk profile in a clinic, and load-driven dullness is not a texture problem to begin with.
