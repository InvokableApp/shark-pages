Your routine

Foundation First

A dull, tired look on rested skin usually isn't a skincare problem. Your answers point at load, which is everything your body is busy processing before any of it reaches your face.

What your answers pointed at

Three lanes. Almost everyone has one that leads, and your plan is ordered by yours.

Systemic loadLead

Stress, sleep debt, sun, alcohol. This is where your answers gathered, and it's the one lane nothing you put on your face can reach.

Surface

Moderate. Keep it simple until the load comes down, because irritated skin reads as duller, not brighter.

Internal supply

Moderate. Supportive for now, and it matters more the longer the load stays high.

Start here, and start free

Ten free habits, starting with the one load you pick

Don't try to change everything at once. Pick the one load you'd genuinely keep changed, and let the rest of the list carry the weight while that one settles.

  1. Week 1

    Pick one load, honestly

    Sleep, alcohol, sun or stress. Choose the one you'd actually keep changed in three months, not the one you feel you should choose. A habit you abandon in week two gives you nothing.

  2. Every day

    Sunscreen, even on the grey days

    Uneven tone and a dull cast are the two things sun exposure reliably produces, and they're the two you described. It's the highest return thirty seconds in the whole plan.

  3. Most days

    Cruciferous vegetables on the plate

    Broccoli, cabbage, Brussels sprouts, kale, cauliflower. This is the food group your body draws DIM from, and it's the one most of us skip. Roasted with oil and salt counts. It doesn't have to be virtuous.

  4. Every day

    Colour on the plate, not in a capsule

    Berries, leafy greens, peppers, tomatoes, anything deeply coloured. Antioxidants from food arrive with everything else the plant had in it, and no capsule reproduces that.

  5. Weeks 1 to 3

    Take the ultra processed food out for three weeks

    Seed oils, refined sugar, anything with a label longer than the food. Three weeks is long enough to notice and short enough that you'll actually finish it. Keep whatever helped, and put back whatever you missed.

  6. Ten minutes daily

    Meditate with a relaxed face

    Jaw unclenched, brow soft, tongue off the roof of your mouth. Most of us hold tension all day in exactly the places we're trying to smooth. Ten minutes is free, you can do it anywhere, and it's the only thing on this page that gets easier the longer you do it.

  7. Weeks 1 to 4

    Two alcohol free nights a week

    Two, not zero. Alcohol costs you sleep quality and hydration on the same night, and those are the two inputs your complexion reads most directly. Two nights is a change women actually keep.

  8. Every morning

    Ten minutes of daylight before screens

    It sets the clock that decides when you get tired, which decides the sleep that decides how you look. Standing outside with your coffee counts.

  9. Week 2

    Then get them out of the rest of the house

    Laundry detergent, cleaning sprays, candles, plug-ins. Your face spends eight hours a night on the sheets you washed and all day in the air you scented, which is more contact time than anything you put on it on purpose.

  10. Today, then day 90

    Take the photo today

    Same window, same time of day, no makeup, no filter. You can't see a twelve week change in a mirror you look into every morning, and without the photo you'll decide nothing happened when something did.

What not to spend money on

Here's what we'd leave on the shelf, and why.

  • Juice cleanses and detox teasYour liver and kidneys already do that job. The teas that produce a visible result do it with a laxative, and what you lose is the water you needed.
  • Brightening actives on irritated skinIf your barrier is already struggling, a brightening acid makes the dullness worse before it makes it better. Get the load down and the routine simple first.
  • A dermaroller, or anything that breaks the skin at homeThe risk at home is nothing like the risk in a clinic, and load driven dullness was never a texture problem to begin with.
