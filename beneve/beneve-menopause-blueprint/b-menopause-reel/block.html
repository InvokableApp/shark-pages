What is actually happening in your body

1

Estrogen and Progesterone Shifts

Mood swings, irregular cycles, irritability, hot flashes.

2

Cortisol Dysregulation

Exhaustion, stubborn belly weight, 2 to 3am wake-ups, no energy for workouts.

3

Blood Sugar Instability

Cravings, brain fog, afternoon slumps, irritable when hungry.

4

Gut and Liver Sluggishness

Puffiness, constipation, skin issues, sluggish digestion.

5

Thyroid and Nutrient Depletion

Cold hands and feet, thinning hair, fatigue. Even with normal labs.

6

Muscle Loss and Metabolic Slowdown

Weakness, weight gain, joint pain, and it speeds up in menopause.
