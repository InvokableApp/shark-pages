Your 3 swaps, in order

  1. Every till

    Receipts

    Thermal paper is usually coated with BPA or BPS and it comes off on your fingers. Skip the printed copy where you can, and wash your hands when you can't. If you handle them all day at work, this one's bigger for you than for most people.

  2. When you restock

    Laundry

    Detergent and dryer sheets sit against your skin for the next sixteen hours and go into the air of your whole house. If you swapped your shampoo and not your detergent, you swapped the smaller one.

  3. Weekly

    Dust

    Household dust is where the plasticizers and flame retardants from your furniture and electronics end up. Use a damp cloth and a vacuum with a real HEPA filter. Dry dusting moves it around.
