Your three top swaps

  1. Tonight

    Stop heating food in plastic

    Heat is what makes plastic leach, and oily food makes it worse. Move it to a plate first.

  2. This week

    Cut the fragrance

    On a label, “fragrance” is a catch-all that doesn't have to list what's in it, and phthalates are commonly in there. Candles, plug-ins, dryer sheets, air freshener. You don't need to replace them. Just stop.

  3. When you can

    Filter your drinking water

    A carbon pitcher is the cheap end. It takes out chlorine and some metals.
