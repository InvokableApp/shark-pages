Your three top swaps

  1. One purchase

    Your shower

    Hot water in a closed room means you're breathing what's in your water, not only drinking it. A shower filter screws onto the arm in about a minute.

  2. Next shop

    Cans

    Tins are usually lined with a plastic coating that contains BPA, and acidic food like tomatoes pulls more of it out. Glass jars, cartons, or dried beans.

  3. Every hot day

    Your car

    A hot car is a sealed plastic box. Crack the windows for a minute before you get in, especially in summer. It costs nothing.
