An endocrine disruptor is an everyday chemical your body mistakes for a hormone.

Your hormones run the slow stuff: energy, sleep, mood, appetite, your cycle. They work in tiny amounts, which is why something that only looks like a hormone is enough to get treated like one.

They're ordinary and they're everywhere. The plastic you reheat lunch in. The coating on a receipt. The word "fragrance" on a label. The lining of a tin. The dust off your own furniture.

Almost none of it is one big exposure. It's small daily contact you never think about. You don't have to throw anything out or buy a cupboard of replacements. You just need to know which ones to do first.
