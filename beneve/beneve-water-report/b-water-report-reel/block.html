Lead, from the required testing

Utilities test taps in their own service area and report the 90th percentile result to the EPA. The federal action level is 15 parts per billion.

PFAS, from EPA's national sampling

Between 2023 and 2025 the EPA had larger systems sampled for 29 PFAS compounds. In 2024 it set a limit of 4 parts per trillion for PFOA and PFOS.

These are your utility's compliance results, not a test of the tap in your kitchen. Private wells are not covered by any of it, because nobody is required to report them.
