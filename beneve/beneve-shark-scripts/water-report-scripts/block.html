How to follow up with your Water Report leads

These leads handed over their address to see what is in their tap water. That is a lot of trust for a first click.

Water Report Leads
Do this, in order 3
  1. 1

    Text them the same day

    They just read something slightly alarming about their own home. Lead with the report, not with a product:

    Hey [Name], this is [Your Name]! Saw you pulled your water report. Did anything on it surprise you?

    Most people are fine, some find one thing worth dealing with. Happy to read it with you if you want a second pair of eyes.

  2. 2

    If they don't answer

    Two days. Give them the one useful thing whether or not they reply:

    Hey [Name], one thing worth knowing: the report shows what the utility measured leaving the plant, not what comes out of your tap. Old pipes in the house change it.

    If yours flagged anything, tell me which line and I will explain what it actually means.

  3. 3

    When they ask what to do about it

    This is the question the funnel earns. There is no Beneve water product, so answer it straight and let the goodwill be the point:

    Depends entirely which contaminant. A carbon filter handles some, does nothing for others, and for a few the honest answer is that your water is fine as it is.

    Tell me which line worried you and I will tell you what it actually means. I am not selling you a filter, this one is just genuinely useful to know.

Tip: There is nothing to sell on this funnel and that is its strength. You are the person who helped somebody read their own water report and asked for nothing. That is what makes the next conversation, about anything else, possible. Do not manufacture an offer here.
