How to talk about the Beneve products

What each one is for, in the words a normal person uses. Claims stay inside what the label says.

Product conversations

Which conversation is this?

Say it like this 2
  1. 1

    Which one, and why that one

    Pick one. A lead who is sold three products buys none of them:

    Renew is the topical one, a face and neck serum built on four peptide technologies. It is the right one if your concern is texture and fine lines.

    Luxe is liquid collagen, one tablespoon a day. That is the one if it is elasticity, hair or nails, because a serum cannot reach any of those. It contains fish (cod), so worth mentioning if they have an allergy.

    Glutathione+ is one capsule a day for the systemic side: antioxidant support and hormone support. It is the one for a dull, tired look on skin that is otherwise fine.

  2. 2

    When they ask which to start with

    The honest answer is usually one, later:

    Start with whichever lane your quiz result led with, and give it a full month on its own before adding anything.

    If you start all three in the same week and something irritates you, you will not know which one to stop.

Say it like this 2
  1. 1

    Keep it about the taste

    Nobody argues about a coffee:

    It is clean coffee, no syrups and nothing that spikes you at eleven. The recipe guide works with it or without it.

    If you want to try it: [your Beneve product link]

  2. 2

    When a sample is requested

    Move fast, samples go cold quicker than anything else:

    Hey [Name], got your sample request. I need to confirm the address before I send it, is [address] still right?

    Text me once it lands and tell me what you think, good or bad.

Tip: Everything on the label is a support claim, not a treatment claim. Say supports, never treats, cures or fixes. If somebody is pregnant, nursing, on medication or under care for anything, tell them to check with their doctor first and mean it.
