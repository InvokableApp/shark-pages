The follow-ups every funnel needs

Objections, silence and referrals. These work whichever funnel the lead came from.

Common situations

Which conversation is this?

Do this, in order 2
  1. 1

    The fourth message, not the second

    Reaching somebody usually takes more attempts than feels comfortable. Change the angle each time:

    Hey [Name], last one from me on this. Did the thing you signed up for actually arrive? Sometimes it lands in promotions.

    If it did and it was not useful, tell me. That is genuinely more helpful to me than silence.

  2. 2

    Then stop, properly

    Leave the door open without another ask:

    No worries at all, I will leave you to it. If you ever want the [guide or result] again just reply to this and I will resend it.

Do this, in order 2
  1. 1

    Ask what is actually behind it

    One question, then be quiet:

    Totally fair. Can I ask what the real hesitation is? If it is the money I would rather just tell you to start with the free plan.

    There is no wrong answer here and I am not going to try to talk you out of it.

  2. 2

    Take the no cleanly

    How you handle a no decides whether they ever come back:

    Completely understand. I will stop bringing it up.

    The free routine still works and it still costs nothing, so please do use it.

Do this, in order 1
  1. 1

    Ask at the moment they say something good

    Not on a schedule. The hour they tell you it worked:

    That is great to hear. Who else do you know who is dealing with the same thing?

    Happy to send them the same free [guide or quiz], no pitch attached, same as you got.

Tip: Most leads in this system are brand new and have never heard of you. Reaching somebody often takes four or five attempts across text and call, and the reps who follow up past the third message are the ones who look lucky.
