How to follow up with your Clean Iced Coffee leads

A recipe guide lead is a low commitment lead. Keep the first message just as light.

Clean Iced Coffee Leads

What did this lead just do?

Do this, in order 2
  1. 1

    Text them the same day

    Ask which one they will try. It is an easy question to answer, which is the point:

    Hey [Name], this is [Your Name]! Sent you the clean iced coffee recipes. Which one are you trying first?

    The cold foam one is the one everyone messages me about.

  2. 2

    If they don't answer

    Two days, and make it about their morning rather than the guide:

    Hey [Name], did the recipes land alright? They are all under two minutes, that was the whole brief.

    If you tell me how you take your coffee I will tell you which one to start with.

Do this, in order 2
  1. 1

    Reply while they are still reading

    Answer the taste question, because it is the only one that matters here:

    Hey [Name], this is [Your Name]. Saw you were looking at the coffee. Happy to answer anything.

    The recipes work with whatever you already have in the cupboard, so no rush. If you do want to try ours, this is my link: [your Beneve coffee link]

  2. 2

    If they go quiet

    Next day, no second pitch:

    Hey [Name], no pressure at all on the coffee. Use the recipes with your usual bag, they were written to work either way.

    Tell me if one of them turns out well.

Once they reply, whichever track they came from 2
  1. 1

    Answer the question, then ask one back

    A reply is not a conversation yet. Give them a straight answer and hand it back:

    Straight answer: [answer their question in one line].

    Can I ask what made you take a look in the first place? It helps me point you at the right thing instead of guessing.

  2. 2

    Check in a week later

    Seven days. Ask about the thing they got, not about buying:

    Hey [Name], it has been about a week. Did you get a chance to use it?

    Honest answer is fine either way. It tells me what to send you next.

Tip: Nobody opts in for a recipe guide because they want to be sold coffee. Ask which recipe, get a reply, and let the product come up on their second or third message, not your first.
