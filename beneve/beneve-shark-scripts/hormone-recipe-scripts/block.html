What did this lead just do?

Do this, in order 3
  1. 1

    Text them the same day

    Send this soon after they opt in, while the guide is still open on their phone:

    Hey [Name], this is [Your Name]! I made the Hormone Lunchbox Recipe Guide you just got in your email, let me know if you have any questions!

    Which lunch are you trying first?

  2. 2

    If they don't answer

    Give it two days. Most people mean to reply and then their week starts:

    Hey [Name], did you get a chance to look at the guide? Let me know which one you try first.

  3. 3

    Once they're talking, invite them to your group

    Only if they're open to it. Skip this if you don't run or belong to a group:

    I also run a Facebook group that is a support system for people who are using holistic methods to improve their life. Would love to have you there! [your Facebook group link]

    When it asks who invited you, just put [Your Name] so I know to let you in!

Do this, in order 2
  1. 1

    Send it while they're still on their phone

    Tap the link in your alert. Their page in the app has your reply ready to send.

    Hey [Name], this is [Your Name]! Here's the supplement guide you asked for, plus the info on Glutathione+. Which goal is the big one for you right now?

  2. 2

    Once they're talking, invite them to your group

    Only if they're open to it:

    I also run a Facebook group where we talk through this stuff every day. Would love to have you there! [your Facebook group link]

    When it asks who invited you, just put [Your Name] so I know to let you in!

If they join your group 2
  1. 1

    Approve them and welcome them by name

    Approve them the same day. Facebook asks new members who invited them, so your name will be on their request. Then post this in the group:

    Welcome [Name]! 👋 Tell us a bit about yourself and what you are working on right now, we all started somewhere.

    Anyone here dealing with the same thing, jump in and say hi.

  2. 2

    Check in a few days later

    Five to seven days after they join. Ask about the guide, not about the group:

    Hey [Name]! Did you get a chance to try anything out of the recipe guide yet?

    Tell me the honest answer, good or bad. It helps me know what to put in the next one.

Tip: Send your own group link, never anyone else's. Paste it into the group invite once, save it in your phone, and every lead after that is a two second reply. Approve member requests daily: somebody who asked to join on Tuesday and got let in on Saturday has already forgotten who you are.
