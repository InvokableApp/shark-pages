How to follow up with your Skin Diagnostic leads

Every lead here already has a free ninety day routine. Your job is to get them to start it.

Skin Diagnostic Leads

What did this lead just do?

Do this, in order 2
  1. 1

    Text them the same day

    Send this while the routine is still open on their phone. Asking which result they got is the fastest way into a real conversation:

    Hey [Name], this is [Your Name]! Saw you just did the skin quiz. Which one did you land on, Surface First, Inside Out, Foundation First or the Full Ritual?

    Whichever it was, the whole ninety day plan on that page is free and you can start it tomorrow morning without buying anything. Take the day one photo today though. That is the part everyone skips and then wishes they had not.

  2. 2

    If they don't answer

    Give it two days. Most people mean to reply and then their week starts:

    Hey [Name], did you get a chance to read your routine? Curious which lane you came out in.

    Tell me which one and I will tell you the single habit on your list that does the most work. It is usually not the one people expect.

Do this, in order 2
  1. 1

    Reply while they are still reading

    You have a few minutes here. Do not pitch. Answer the question they were about to ask:

    Hey [Name], this is [Your Name]. Saw you were reading about [Renew / Luxe / Glutathione+]. Ask me anything about it, I use these myself.

    Straight answer though: the free habits on your result page do most of the work in the first month. The product speeds it up, it does not replace it. Have you started the routine yet?

  2. 2

    If they go quiet

    Next day. The honest version lands better than a second pitch:

    Hey [Name], genuinely no pressure on the product. If you only do one thing this week, make it the sunscreen every morning and the protein at breakfast. Both free, both beat anything in a bottle.

    If you do decide you want it, here is my link: [your Beneve product link]

Do this, in order 2
  1. 1

    Reply within minutes

    Do not congratulate them on buying, they may not have finished. Ask whether it went through:

    Hey [Name], this is [Your Name]! Just saw you clicked through. Did it go through alright, or did you get stuck at checkout?

    If you have not ordered yet, ask me first. I would rather you get the right one for your result than the one everybody buys.

  2. 2

    Next day, if nothing came through

    Give them an easy out. It keeps the lead alive instead of ending it:

    Hey [Name], did you get sorted? If the site was being difficult, say so and I will send you a direct link.

    And if you changed your mind, that is completely fine. The ninety day routine still works without it, that was always the deal.

Once they reply, whichever track they came from 2
  1. 1

    Give them one habit, not the whole list

    The plans have seven or eight habits. Handing over all of them is how someone does none of them:

    Since you came out [their result], start with the first habit on your list and run it on its own for two weeks. Just that one.

    Do not start three things at once. If you do, you will not know which one worked, and that is the whole reason the plan is ordered.

  2. 2

    Check in at day fourteen

    Two weeks in. Ask about the habit, not about buying anything:

    Hey [Name], two weeks in. Did you stick with it? Be honest, good or bad. It tells me what to send you next.

    If you took the day one photo, do not look at it yet. Wait for day ninety. Skin changes too slowly to see in a mirror you look in every morning.

Tip: The result page promises the routine works without buying anything, so do not open with a product. Ask which result they got, give them one habit, and let the product come up when they ask. A rep who leads with the free plan gets a reply. A rep who leads with a bottle gets ignored. Save these in your phone once and every lead after that is a two second answer.
