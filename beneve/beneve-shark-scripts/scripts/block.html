How to talk about the Beneve opportunity

For anyone asking what you actually do, however they got to the question.

Opportunity conversations

Which conversation is this?

Do this, in order 2
  1. 1

    Answer in two sentences

    Long answers sound like a pitch. Short ones sound like a job:

    I share wellness products I actually use, and I get paid a share of what sells. Beneve puts 65% of every dollar into compensation, which is why it is worth doing properly.

    There are thirteen ways to earn in the plan, but honestly most people start with one and add later.

  2. 2

    Send the page, not a pitch

    Let them read it alone. People decide about money in private:

    Easier to read it than have me describe it: [your Beneve opportunity link]

    Have a look whenever, and ask me the awkward questions after. I would rather you ask them than not join because nobody did.

Do this, in order 2
  1. 1

    Do not dodge the question

    Denying the category loses the room. Name what is actually different:

    Fair question, and yes it is a referral model. What is different here is written down: 65% of every dollar allocated to compensation, and an Influencer Bill of Rights that says the company cannot touch your social channels or your audience.

    You can read both before you talk to me again if you want.

  2. 2

    Give them the exit

    Offering an easy no is what makes a yes believable:

    If it is not for you that is genuinely fine, and I am not going to keep messaging you about it.

    You are welcome to just use the products, plenty of people do.

Tip: Never quote an income figure, promise a result, or describe this as passive. Say what the plan pays and let people do their own arithmetic. Compliance aside, the people who join off a hyped number are the ones who quit first.
