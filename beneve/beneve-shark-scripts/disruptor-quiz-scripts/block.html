How to follow up with your Disruptor Diagnostic leads

Three results, three very different conversations. Check which one they got before you type anything.

Disruptor Diagnostic Leads

What did this lead just do?

Do this, in order 2
  1. 1

    Text them the same day

    Lead with how easy the first swaps are, never with how bad the score was:

    Hey [Name], this is [Your Name]! Saw you did the disruptor quiz. Do not let the result put you off, Starting Out just means the easy wins are all still on the table.

    Pick one swap off your list this week. One. The plastic one is usually the fastest.

  2. 2

    If they don't answer

    Two days. Give them the single swap so the text is useful even unanswered:

    Hey [Name], if you only do one thing: stop heating food in plastic. That single change does more than most of the rest combined.

    Tell me how you get on and I will send you the next one.

Do this, in order 2
  1. 1

    Text them the same day

    Acknowledge what they already do before adding anything:

    Hey [Name], this is [Your Name]! You came out Partway There, which means you have already cut the obvious stuff. Nice.

    The ones left on your list are the hidden ones. Which of them surprised you?

  2. 2

    If they don't answer

    Two days:

    Hey [Name], the one most people in your bracket miss is receipts and food packaging, not cleaning products.

    Worth thirty seconds of your week. Tell me if you already had that one covered.

Do this, in order 2
  1. 1

    Text them the same day

    Treat them as a peer. These leads convert to the opportunity more often than to a product:

    Hey [Name], this is [Your Name]! You scored Well Ahead, which almost nobody does. You clearly already look into this stuff.

    Genuinely curious, what got you started on it?

  2. 2

    If the conversation opens up

    People who already live this way are the ones worth telling about the opportunity:

    Since you already do this, worth mentioning: I get paid to share the products I would use anyway. That is the whole model.

    No pitch, happy to just send you what it looks like if you are curious: [your Beneve opportunity link]

Once they reply, whichever track they came from 2
  1. 1

    Answer the question, then ask one back

    A reply is not a conversation yet. Give them a straight answer and hand it back:

    Straight answer: [answer their question in one line].

    Can I ask what made you take a look in the first place? It helps me point you at the right thing instead of guessing.

  2. 2

    Check in a week later

    Seven days. Ask about the thing they got, not about buying:

    Hey [Name], it has been about a week. Did you get a chance to use it?

    Honest answer is fine either way. It tells me what to send you next.

Tip: The result is not a grade and should never be delivered like one. A lead who feels judged for their score does not reply, and Starting Out is the biggest group you will get.
