Where this number comes from
  1. The baseline. About 35 ml of water a day for every kilogram you weigh, a common adult figure.
  2. Less what you eat. About 20 percent comes off, because food carries water too, so the comparison is drinks against drinks.
  3. Plus the sweat. Roughly half a liter an hour of hard effort, and up to a third more when it's hot. That half liter is our own figure, picked near the low end of the range commonly reported, so this leans conservative.
  4. The salt. About 900 mg of sodium in every liter of sweat, a commonly reported average. People vary a lot.

Then we compare it to the bottles you told us about. It's arithmetic on what you entered, so it describes a body of that size on a day like that. It doesn't know anything about you.

A general estimate, not medical advice, and not an assessment of any individual. Talk to your doctor about what's right for you, especially if you're pregnant, take medication or have been told to limit fluids or sodium.
