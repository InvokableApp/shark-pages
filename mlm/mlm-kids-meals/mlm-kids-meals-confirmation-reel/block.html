Three ways to win over little eaters

  1. 1
    Make it fun to hold. Nuggets, sticks, wraps, and mini anything beat a pile on a plate.
  2. 2
    Offer it more than once. Serve a new food next to two they already like, with no pressure to finish.
  3. 3
    Bring them into the kitchen. Kids who stir, sprinkle, or crack an egg are far more likely to eat the result.
