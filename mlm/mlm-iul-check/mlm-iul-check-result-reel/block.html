Ask these before you sign any IUL

  1. What are all the fees and charges in the first 10 years?
  2. How big is the surrender charge, and how many years does it last?
  3. What's the cap, and can the company change it?
  4. What happens if I take a loan and don't pay it back?
  5. Which numbers in this illustration are guaranteed, and which aren't?
