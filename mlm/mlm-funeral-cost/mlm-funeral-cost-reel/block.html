What a funeral costs today

A funeral with a viewing and burial: $8,300. With a viewing and cremation: $6,280. Those are the national middle prices, before the cemetery plot, opening the grave and the headstone.

Source: National Funeral Directors Association, 2023

What Social Security pays

A one-time $255, and only to a spouse or child who qualifies. The rest comes out of savings, a credit card, or a fundraiser.

Source: Social Security Administration
