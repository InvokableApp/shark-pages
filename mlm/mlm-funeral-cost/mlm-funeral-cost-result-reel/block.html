Three things that help, whatever you decide

1

Write down what you want

Burial or cremation, the songs, who to call. Then tell one person where the note is.

2

Ask for the price list

By law a funeral home has to give you an itemized price list, and you only pay for what you pick.

3

Know what's in place

Any policy, any prepaid plan, and where the papers are. A policy nobody can find doesn't help.

1 of 3

If health is the worry

Some policies ask health questions and some don't. The ones that don't usually have a waiting period: if death comes in the first two years, most pay back only what was paid in, plus a little. Ask about that before you sign anything.
