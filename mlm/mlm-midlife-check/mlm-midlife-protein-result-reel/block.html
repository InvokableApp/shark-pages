Five free habits for your strength

Every morning

Twenty five to thirty grams of protein at breakfast

Real protein, not processed. Most of us put the carbs at breakfast and leave the protein until dinner, so breakfast is the one worth fixing first.

  • What thirty grams looks like. Three eggs with a bit of cheese. A can of tuna. Greek yogurt with seeds. Leftover chicken.
  • What doesn't get close. A bowl of cereal is about six grams, and toast with jam is under five.
  • Keep the coffee. Have it with breakfast, not in place of it.
Every evening

Protein at dinner too

A palm-sized portion at dinner: chicken, fish, eggs, beans, tofu or lean beef.

  • Cook once, use twice. A tray of chicken thighs on Sunday covers three dinners.
Every morning

Twenty minutes of walking or stretching

First thing, before the day gets going.

  • Same time every day. It sticks faster when you don't have to decide.
Twice a week

Lift something heavy

Lifting is the strongest signal your body gets to build and hold muscle, and it puts the protein from breakfast to use.

  • Two sessions you keep beat five you dread.
  • Heavy for you. The last two reps of each set should be hard. If they aren't, the weight is too light.
  • Where to start. Squat, hinge, push, pull, carry. A trainer's intro session or any beginner's program covers all five.
  • Keep the walks. Add the weights on top.
Every day

Protein and fiber at lunch

A lunch that is mostly carbs lifts you and drops you an hour later. Build it on protein and vegetables so the afternoon holds.

  • The formula. A vegetable base, a palm of protein, a dressing with fat and acid, something crunchy.
  • Pack it the night before. Your bonus guide has 10 of them, and they all hold cold.
