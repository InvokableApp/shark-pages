Five free habits for your nights

Every morning

Ten minutes of daylight

Outside, or at an open window, within an hour of waking up. Morning light helps set the body clock that decides when you feel sleepy at night.

  • Take your coffee outside. It's the easiest way to make it stick.
  • Cloudy mornings count. Daylight on a grey day is still far brighter than a lit kitchen.
Midday

A five minute pause

Five minutes of slow breathing or stretching, and a break from screens. An evening is calmer when the day had a gap in it.

  • Put it in your calendar. Right after lunch works for most people.
  • Step away from the screen. A walk around the block counts.
Every evening

Finish dinner three hours before bed

Keep dinner light, and finish eating three hours before you go to sleep.

  • Work back from bedtime. In bed at 10:30 means dinner done by 7:30.
Every evening

Screens off an hour before bed

Phone in another room, TV off. Swap in a warm bath, a book or some gentle yoga.

  • Charge your phone outside the bedroom. A cheap alarm clock solves the "but it's my alarm" problem.
  • Keep a book on the pillow. It's there when the phone isn't.
Every night

The same bedtime

Weekends too. Aim for 7 to 8 hours.

  • Count back from your alarm. Up at 6:30 means lights out by 10:30.
  • Keep it on Saturday. A late Saturday makes Sunday night harder.
