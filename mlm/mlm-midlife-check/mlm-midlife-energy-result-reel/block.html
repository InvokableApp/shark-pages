Your energy

Also playing a part
  • Your sleep. A short night makes any afternoon dip worse, so keep an eye on your bedtime too.

Five free habits for your afternoons

Every morning

Stop skipping breakfast

Coffee on an empty stomach and nothing until lunch is a long stretch to run on. Eat something real in the morning, with protein in it.

  • Easy ones. Eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese or last night's leftover chicken.
  • Keep the coffee. Have it with breakfast, not in place of it.
Every day

Protein and fiber at lunch

A lunch that is mostly carbs lifts you and drops you an hour later. Build it on protein and vegetables so the afternoon holds.

  • The formula. A vegetable base, a palm of protein, a dressing with fat and acid, something crunchy.
  • Pack it the night before. Your bonus guide has 10 of them, and they all hold cold.
By 3pm

16 to 24oz of water

Water or herbal tea, between breakfast and 3pm.

  • Fill one big bottle in the morning. Finish it by 3.
  • Mineral water or a squeeze of lemon if plain water is the reason you skip it.
After lunch

A ten minute walk

A movement break after lunch, ten to fifteen minutes of walking.

  • Take a call on your feet. Or walk to get your coffee instead of making it.
Over a few weeks

Less sugar and ultra-processed food

Not all at once. Swap one packaged snack a day for something real, and build from there.

  • Easy swaps. A boiled egg, an apple with peanut butter, a handful of nuts.
