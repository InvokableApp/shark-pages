PMI, mortgage protection and term life: what's what

1

PMI

Protects the lender if payments stop. Pays nothing to your family.

2

Mortgage protection insurance

Life insurance meant to pay off the mortgage if you die. Some also pay if you're too sick or hurt to work. Some pay less as your balance goes down.

3

Term life insurance

Pays your family a set amount if you die during the term. They can put it toward the house or anything else.
