Your three top swaps

Start with these three

Tonight

Stop heating food in plastic

Heat is what makes plastic leach, and oily food makes it worse. Move it to a plate first.

  • Plate first, container after it cools. That's the whole swap, and it costs nothing.
  • Source: NIEHS, Bisphenol A.
This week

Cut the fragrance

On a label, "fragrance" is a catch-all that doesn't have to list what's in it, and phthalates are commonly in there. Candles, plug-ins, dryer sheets, air freshener.

  • You don't need to replace them. Just stop. Open a window, or simmer citrus peel if you want the room to smell like something.
  • Source: EPA, Phthalates.
When you can

Filter your drinking water

A carbon pitcher is the cheap end. It takes out chlorine and some metals.

  • Buy it by the certification, not the brand. Look for NSF/ANSI 53 on the box. NSF/ANSI 42 on its own is taste and smell only.
  • Source: EPA, Drinking Water Standards; CDC, Choosing a Home Water Filter.
