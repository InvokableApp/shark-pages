  1. 1

    Most of your leads. Text this soon after they opt in:

    Hey [Name], this is [Your Name]! I made the Hormone Lunchbox Recipe Guide you just got in your email, let me know if you have any questions!

    BTW, I also run a Facebook group that is a support system for people who are using holistic methods to improve their life. Would love to have you there! [your Facebook group link]

  2. 2

    They clicked to join your Facebook group

    You get a text the moment this happens. Reply while they are still on their phone:

    Hey [Name], this is [Your Name], just saw you clicked to join the Facebook group I run. I'll check now and add you.

    Feel free to reach out to me here if you have any questions about the Hormone Lunchbox Recipe Guide. Would love for you to introduce yourself in the group as well, we love meeting new people and hearing their stories!

Tip: Swap [Name], [Your Name] and [your Facebook group link] every time. Paste your own group link once, save the script in your phone, and it is a two second reply after that.
