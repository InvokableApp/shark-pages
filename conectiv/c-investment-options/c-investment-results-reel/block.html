Foundations

Educational insights

Diversification

Spreading capital across different assets can help reduce the impact of any single investment underperforming.

Risk vs Reward

Higher potential returns are generally paired with higher potential losses. Understanding this trade-off is foundational.

Compound Growth

Returns that are reinvested may generate their own returns over time, which can matter more the longer money stays invested.

Volatility

Volatility describes how much an investment’s price moves. High volatility means bigger swings in value in either direction.

Liquidity

Liquidity refers to how easily an asset can be converted to cash without a large price impact.

Time Horizon

Your time horizon is how long you expect to hold an investment before needing the money. Longer horizons can change how risk is perceived.

Dollar Cost Averaging

Investing a fixed amount on a regular schedule spreads purchases over time, regardless of price.

Building an Investment Plan

A written plan describing goals, timeline, risk tolerance, and contribution amount may help make decisions more consistent over time.
