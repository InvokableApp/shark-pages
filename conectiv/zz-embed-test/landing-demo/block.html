01 The idea

Most people quit before they ever feel anything.

Not because they are lazy, and not because the product was wrong. They stacked six new habits on a week that was already full, missed three days, and decided the whole thing was not for them.

Conectiv is built the other way around. One change that fits the week you already have. Something you can keep doing when the week goes sideways, because it will.

You do not need a new personality. You need a routine that survives a busy week.
02 What makes it different
i

Products that earn their place

Short ingredient lists you can read out loud. If it does not do something you can feel within a month, it does not belong in the routine.

ii

A path, not a pitch

Plenty of people never do anything but buy the products, and that is a completely fine place to stop. The income side is there when you want it.

iii

Someone who actually answers

You get a real person, not a support queue. Ask the awkward questions. That is what they are there for.

  1. 1
