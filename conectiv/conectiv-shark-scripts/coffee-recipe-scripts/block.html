How to follow up with your Coffee Recipe Guide leads

Use these scripts immediately after receiving a new recipe guide lead.

Coffee Recipe Guide Leads
Watch first 1

Watch this before you start calling.

Do this, in order 3
  1. 1

    Call them

    They probably won't pick up, so leave this voicemail:

    Hey [Name], it's [Your Name]!

    I saw you requested our free coffee recipe guide. I'm going to text you the link now so you have it handy and can check out all the recipes.

    Also, since you're obviously a coffee person, I can send you some info on a coffee I've been really excited about lately. It's an easy swap for your normal cup of coffee and has some pretty interesting wellness-focused ingredients.

    If you're curious about it, just reply back to my text and I'll send you the details. No pressure at all!

    If you don't see my text, check your Unknown Senders or Spam folder. On an iPhone, look for the filter or menu icon in the top right corner of your Messages app. On an Android, look for the three-dot menu. You should be able to find any filtered messages there, which is where my text might be.

    Okay, that's all for now! Let me know if you got the text. Talk soon!

  2. 2

    Text them

    As soon as you hang up, send this:

    Hey [Name], it's [Your Name]! 👋 Just left you a quick voicemail. Here's the link to the coffee recipe guide you requested so you have it handy:

    https://assets.cdn.filesafe.space/uNxituXahyZtAIqwdVJI/media/096d68de-be8c-4d73-a37b-8e5e5358c840.pdf

    If your morning coffee gives you a boost but you end up dragging by the afternoon or reaching for another cup, you might want to check this out. I've been sharing a premium coffee that's designed around a smoother coffee experience while adding protein, collagen, superfoods and adaptogens to your daily cup.

    It's a really simple swap, and honestly, I think coffee drinkers should know about it.

    Want the info? Just reply COFFEE and I'll send it over.

  3. 3

    Follow up in 24 hrs

    If they don't respond within 24 hours, text again:

    [Name]! Just following up on the above! 👆 Were you able to get the recipe guide? Want some info on the coffee with health benefits that I mentioned? Shoot me a text back - I can hook you up!

Tip: Personalize [Name] and [Your Name] every time. The recipe guide link is already in the text script - just hit Copy script and send it as-is.
