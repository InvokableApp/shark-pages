How to follow up with your Travel Quiz leads

Use these scripts immediately after receiving a new travel quiz lead.

Travel Quiz Leads
Watch first 1

Watch this before you start calling.

Do this, in order 3
  1. 1

    Call them

    They probably won't pick up, so leave this voicemail:

    Hey [Name], it's [Your Name]!

    I saw you took our travel quiz, so I wanted to reach out and send you some info related to what you were looking for.

    Also, based on your interest in travel, I thought you might be interested in something that can help you find discounted rates on hotels, accommodations, and other travel.

    It's a pretty cool way to potentially save on trips you're already planning, so if you're curious, just reply back to my text and I'll send you the details. No pressure at all!

    If you don't see my text, check your Unknown Senders or Spam folder. On an iPhone, look for the filter or menu icon in the top right corner of your Messages app. On an Android, look for the three-dot menu. You should be able to find any filtered messages there, which is where my text might be.

    Okay, that's all for now! Let me know if you got the text. Talk soon!

  2. 2

    Text them

    As soon as you hang up, send this:

    Hey [Name], it's [Your Name]! 👋 Just left you a quick voicemail. I saw you took our travel quiz, so I wanted to send you some info related to what you were looking for.

    Based on your interest in travel, I thought you might be interested in something that can help you find discounted rates on hotels, accommodations, and other travel.

    It's a pretty cool way to potentially save on trips you're already planning. If you're curious, just reply TRAVEL and I'll send you the details!

  3. 3

    Follow up in 24 hrs

    If they don't respond within 24 hours, text again:

    [Name]! Just following up on the above! 👆 Were you able to check out the info I sent over? If you're interested in potentially saving on hotels, accommodations, and other travel, shoot me a text back and I'll send you the details!

Tip: Personalize [Name] and [Your Name] every time. The travel follow-up copy is already in the scripts - just hit Copy script and send it as-is.
